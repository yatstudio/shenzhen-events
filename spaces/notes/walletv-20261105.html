一、结论先行

  1. 全场对短期行情的共识是「回调未完、不是熊市」：教主认为当前只是 4 小时级别回调、未到日线级别，是可预见的结构性回调；茄哥认为是标准 ABC 回调，C 浪会更深、可能砸出「黄金坑」；但对坑有多深分歧极大——Michael 认为 7 万就足以止跌、6 万开头很难再见，唐长老则看未来半年偏空、比特币到 5 万开头可能性很高、到 4 万也不意外。
  2. 钱包安全是全场最一致的实操共识：现货放真正物理隔离的冷钱包，交易资本不要全押在中心化交易所；自托管钱包（以 WalletV 为代表的聚合钱包）被多位嘉宾视为交易资本的新去处，可分走 20%–40%。
  3. 中期选举本身被老唐「祛魅」：他认为市场同时高估了中期选举的影响、高估了共和党对加密的支持、也高估了民主党的打压力度；真正值得盯的不是选举，而是 AI 私募债这个可能比肩 2008 年次贷危机的雷。
  4. AI 交易的场内判断很克制：AI 不能创造策略，只能执行策略、做风控；真正有意义的是基于成熟交易体系训练的个人 AI；DeFi 狙击手自述 9 月在 AI 策略上跑了 5 万多美元、目前约 3 万美元在跑、整体盈利，但始终保留人工入口。
  5. 直播本身就是一面行情镜子：开场时大饼还在 7.8 万美元上方，节目进行中跌破 7.8 万，收尾时教主称已跌破她刚给出的 7.6 万美元位置——嘉宾一边聊一边目睹支撑位告破。

二、开场与赞助：WalletV 积分规则（Adam 介绍）

  • 当晚福利：上麦提问的 5 位听众各得 200 积分，评论区留言的 10 位各得 100 积分，中奖率自述很高。
  • 积分用途（Adam 原话）：可兑换 VIP 等级，提升交易返佣与获得奖励的概率；可参与 Launchpad 功能，免费获得代币空投；WalletV 代币即将发行（TGE），持有积分的用户届时可获得相应代币。
  • WalletV 定位：聚合 Polymarket、Hyperliquid、Aster、xStocks 的自托管钱包；当晚新上线聚合 paper trade 的 1000 倍合约（CFD 模式，见第六节）。

三、行情总览：教主的判断

  • 背景：上周币安安全事件后市场大波动，比特币带头下跌；但 11 月 4 日市场走出一波不错的上涨，前天（11 月 3 日前后）她开始做多，认为是日线级别的「二买」机会、非常难得。
  • 当前回调：只是 4 小时级别回调，还没到日线级别；一是受美股下跌带动，二是前期底部没夯实，一般要经过「二次探底」才会形成止跌形态，所以回调是正常的、可以预见的。
  • 她的操作：昨天信号提示多单可以止盈，她在原来盈利的位置设了止损直接离场，当天被粉丝骂「怎么又拿不住」；她回应：该走还要走，避免回撤。
  • 底线判断：这不是进熊市；除非再来一次更大的黑天鹅——她个人认为不太会有，交易所出事已经算是很大的事了（类比 2022 年 FTX 事件）。

四、币安事件后，流动性往哪走（Adam 主讲）

  • 定性：对全行业是利空，不论交易所还是钱包；大部分用户不懂技术细节，只会觉得「连币安这么专业的机构都会出事，还有谁不会出事」，信心恢复需要时间与教育。
  • 观察到的迁移：用户先在交易所内部跳来跳去（币安→OK→其他交易所）；Coinbase 股价大跌后，也有人担心它会不会是下一个。
  • 交易资本的新选择：现货可以放冷钱包，但交易资本放冷钱包不现实（平仓时 U 盘不在身边就平不掉）；自托管钱包提供了一个新选项——在 Hyperliquid 最高 40 倍、Aster 100–200 倍、paper trade 1000 倍（CFD、无滑点），比特币、以太坊等主流币深度足够。
  • 他的估计：随着教育到位，自托管钱包承接交易资本的 20%–30% 甚至 30%–40% 是合理的，目的很简单：分散黑客风险；而且不用 KYC、不会被卡币、没有风控，DEX 也无法冻结任何资金。
  • 手机钱包的安全性：iPhone 的安全级别不低于一些小的硬件钱包厂商（随机数按苹果硬件标准），但肯定比不过真正的硬件钱包。

五、钱包怎么放才安全（Michael 主讲，教主补充）

  • Michael 的唯一原则：冷钱包一定不能联网。凡是宣称「绝对安全、可以联网升级」的都不是冷钱包；现在 AI 这么厉害，联网就一定有漏洞，只能相信物理隔离。
  • 不要买二手冷钱包，不要把冷钱包寄回厂商换新：他举例某神冷钱包（小众，只有中文客服），以太坊 POW 转 POS 时全系列存不了 POS 的 ETH，厂商让用户把钱包寄回折价换新——他说宁可砸碎也不寄回去贪小便宜。
  • 真实案例（Michael 转述社群经历）：一位大哥早年误把 600 个 ETH 买成 600 个 BTC（当时 ETH 才 90 多美元），后来买了 1000 多台 BTC 矿机；2023 年其 600 个 BTC 被盗——其财务把私钥存在 U 盘、微信收藏里也存了一份、还发邮件给了在美国的女儿。Michael 的结论是「活该被盗」：你足够安全，资产就足够安全。
  • 分层建议（Michael 原话）：资产没到七八百个比特币就别考虑冷钱包，直接放币安、OK 等头部交易所——「要脸、赔得起」，人家还有更多的钱没赚；多签、多钱包分散也是一种保护；冷钱包的要义是「只进不出」，前提是真正物理隔离。
  • 他刚在新加坡买了一台 iPhone 5，打算手搓一个「半冷钱包」；他认为不少人选择这种方式，毕竟 100% 在自己手里、完全不联网。
  • 教主补充：她 2021 年玩 DeFi 时被盗过好几次，对链上安全有点怕；但她是高频交易，没办法不把钱放交易所，否则开单很麻烦。

六、捕捉波动：1000 倍杠杆与 AI 交易

1000 倍杠杆是怎么实现的（听众提问，Adam 回答）

  • 不是 WalletV 自己提供 1000 倍，而是聚合了 paper trade 这个项目；本质是 CFD：没有滑点、没有手续费，赚钱时平台收约 2% 提成，亏钱时送 paper token、可在 WalletV 质押分币。
  • 适用场景：只适合赌 2 个百分点以内的小波动、大仓位，成本远低于中心化交易所；波动超过 2 个点就不划算。
  • 他的原话：波动在 2 个点以内的博弈最合适，「它没有深度概念，波动直接定价」。

AI 交易，普通人怎么用（听众老 A 提问）

  • DeFi 狙击手：普通人完全可以用豆包、ChatGPT、Claude 这类通用模型，拿 6 小时、日 K、1 小时 K 线去问趋势、问拿多还是拿空，「给出的判断其实还蛮靠谱的」；他自己在 WalletV 里跑 AI 交易（动量策略），也用 Claude Code、Codex 自己开发了一个 Meme 币扫链雷达「MemeDog」，目前半自动（AI 推荐、人工点单），全自动还在测试——打狗风险太大，大多会归零。
  • 唐长老：AI 做技术面分析一般；基本面分析能力理论上应强于技术面，但实际决策端遗漏还很多，普通人的决策力不会因此顺利提高；真正有意义的是基于成熟交易体系、高胜率交易员训练出来的个人 AI，「教主搞一个 AI 训练得不错的话，我是肯定付费的」。
  • Michael：AI 不能创造策略，只能执行策略、帮他做风控——提高不爆仓的概率、严格执行止盈止损，「人是有贪欲的」，比如跌到 75000 想等 74000 再进，AI 到 79999999（指止盈价位）就已经下手了。
  • 教主：她从今年 4 月开始搞自己的策略，在推特发过信号策略，但只是信号、不能自己跑，正在做信号自动执行；手动交易受情绪影响，AI 做量化套利很好，但捕捉单边趋势行情时可能错过；未来发展会很快，「希望再能给我们人类交易员留点活路」。
  • DeFi 狙击手的实盘（自述）：9 月份在 AI 策略上跑了 5 万多美元，现在约 3 万美元在跑，整体盈利；预测市场也用 AI：收集新闻、跟踪胜率高的钱包做参考，只投了 1000U；把教主的缠论策略做进 AI 的事做了一半没做完，「一个 Agent 真正跑起来要两三个月、有时将近半年」；始终保留人工入口，「半年不看它也不现实」。

WalletV 的量化模型（Adam 收尾介绍）

  • 给不会写 prompt 的小白准备的两个版本：趋势跟踪、动量指标，可调几个参数；回测夏普比率超过 1.5，过去一年行情回测收益约 340%（Adam 自述数字，「回测不能代表未来」）。
  • 本质不是 AI 交易，而是 AI 研究框架下的量化交易；他的说法是把量化基金 60%–70% 的能力交给散户使用。

八、中期选举与美股：老唐的基本面长文（全场最悲观的声音）

  • 中期选举规律不明显：很多人过分高估了中期选举的影响——既高估了共和党对加密的支持力度，也高估了民主党的打压力度；有很多东西是中期选举决定不了的。
  • Michael 补充的历史统计（自述）：过去三次中期选举后一个月，比特币两跌一涨，跌幅约 20%、19%，涨幅约 30% 多；但拉一年看三次都是涨的，最高涨了一倍。
  • AI 股下跌的两个原因（老唐）：一是民主党拿下德州等建数据中心的州，后面对 AI 数据中心建设的环保与规范会趋严、建设放慢，上游硬件需求不如原来预期；二是 Anthropic 财报显示 AI 收入增速二季度 116%、三季度已掉到 46%，如果四季度掉到 20% 就更糟；10 月中旬 AI 股票就在跌，主因是业绩远不如预期——OpenAI 原来 2–2.5 万亿美元的估值预期已被压到约 1.5 万亿美元，且今年不发 IPO，「其中肯定是业绩增速有问题」；Anthropic 自己都承认 AI 对做产品效率的提升远不如预期。
  • 最大的黑天鹅（老唐原话重点）：如果民主党政策跟上，AI 相关私募债市场可能出问题，进而引发美国私募债爆雷，「这个雷非常大，可以比肩 2008 年的次贷危机」；美股至少回调 30%，比特币到 4–5 万「毫不意外」。
  • 私募债规模（老唐自述数字）：疫情时美国私募债总规模才 500 亿美元，现在已接近 2 万亿美元，其中至少 3000 亿美元投入非上市 AI 基础设施；基于这些私募债又做了保险、衍生品，规模「无法计算」；他 2025 年 10 月开 Space 时就提出过这个担忧。
  • 他的时间判断：未来 3–6 个月比较看空；美国 QE 如果来，倾向于明年二季度或三季度开始放水，所以上涨趋势到来的概率比较大的是明年二季度、三季度往后。
  • 茄哥的美股观察：周一（11 月 3 日前后）他和某老师做美股专栏时就讲「星期一反弹赶紧跑、不反弹也要跑」；特斯拉当天跌超 10%，存储股（闪迪等「存储三傻」）血流成河；消息面是主导，恐慌踩踏是机构在做；热力图上消费、民生板块很坚挺——沃尔玛他一个多月前就买了，「很坚挺」；短线做空水位高很理想；时间窗口上一直认为 10 月 10 日可能有反转，但只是反弹。

IV. After the Binance Incident, Where Does Liquidity Go? (led by Adam)

  • Framing: it is bearish for the whole industry, exchanges and wallets alike; most users don't understand the technical details and will simply feel "if even an institution as professional as Binance can have an incident, who can't?" — restoring confidence takes time and education.
  • Observed migration: users first hop around inside exchanges (Binance → OK → other exchanges); after Coinbase's stock price fell sharply, some also worried whether it might be next.
  • A new option for trading capital: spot can sit in a cold wallet, but trading capital in a cold wallet is impractical (if the USB drive isn't with you when you need to close a position, you can't close it); self-custody wallets offer a new option — up to 40x on Hyperliquid, 100–200x on Aster, 1000x on paper trade (CFD, no slippage), with sufficient depth in majors such as Bitcoin and Ethereum.
  • His estimate: as education catches up, it is reasonable for self-custody wallets to take on 20%–30%, or even 30%–40%, of trading capital; the purpose is simple — diversify hacker risk. And there is no KYC, no coin-withholding, no risk controls, and a DEX cannot freeze any funds.
  • Mobile wallet security: an iPhone's security level is no lower than that of some small hardware-wallet vendors (random numbers follow Apple's hardware standards), though it certainly cannot match a real hardware wallet.

V. How Should Wallets Be Stored to Be Safe? (led by Michael, with Jiaozhu adding)

  • Michael's only principle: a cold wallet must never connect to the internet. Anything that claims to be "absolutely safe and upgradable online" is not a cold wallet; with AI as capable as it is now, going online means there will definitely be vulnerabilities — the only thing to trust is physical isolation.
  • Don't buy second-hand cold wallets, and don't mail a cold wallet back to the vendor for a trade-in: he cited a certain obscure cold wallet (a niche product with only Chinese-language customer service) whose entire line could not store POS ETH after Ethereum's POW-to-POS transition; the vendor asked users to mail their wallets back for a discounted replacement — he said he would rather smash it than send it back for a small bargain.
  • A real case (Michael relaying a community member's experience): a man early on mistakenly bought 600 BTC instead of 600 ETH (when ETH was only a bit over $90), later bought more than 1,000 BTC miners; in 2023 his 600 BTC were stolen — his finance staff had stored the private key on a USB drive, kept another copy in WeChat favorites, and had also emailed it to his daughter in the US. Michael's verdict was "he deserved to be robbed": if you are safe enough, your assets are safe enough.
  • Tiered advice (in Michael's words): if your assets haven't reached seven or eight hundred Bitcoin, don't bother with a cold wallet — just keep funds on top exchanges like Binance and OK, which "care about their reputation and can afford to pay out", and still have more money yet to earn; multisig and spreading funds across multiple wallets is also a form of protection; the essence of a cold wallet is "in only, never out", premised on true physical isolation.
  • He had just bought an iPhone 5 in Singapore, intending to hand-build a "semi-cold wallet"; he believes quite a few people choose this approach — after all, it is 100% in your own hands and never connects to the internet.
  • Jiaozhu added: she was robbed several times playing DeFi in 2021 and is somewhat afraid of on-chain security; but she trades at high frequency and cannot avoid keeping money on exchanges, otherwise opening positions is too much trouble.

VI. Capturing Volatility: 1000x Leverage and AI Trading

How is 1000x leverage achieved? (audience question, answered by Adam)

  • WalletV does not itself provide 1000x; it aggregates the paper trade project. In essence it is a CFD: no slippage, no fees; when you profit, the platform takes about a 2% cut, and when you lose it gives you paper tokens that can be staked on WalletV to earn distributions.
  • Applicable scenarios: only suitable for betting on small moves within 2 percentage points with large position sizes, at a cost far below centralized exchanges; once volatility exceeds 2 points it is no longer cost-effective.
  • In his words: betting on moves within 2 points is the best fit — "it has no concept of depth; volatility is priced directly".

AI trading — how can ordinary people use it? (question from audience member Lao A)

  • DeFi Sniper: ordinary people can perfectly well use general-purpose models such as Doubao, ChatGPT and Claude, feeding them 6-hour, daily and 1-hour candles to ask about trend and whether to hold long or short — "the judgments they give are actually quite reliable". He himself runs AI trading (a momentum strategy) inside WalletV, and also used Claude Code and Codex to develop his own meme-coin chain-scanning radar "MemeDog", currently semi-automatic (AI recommends, a human clicks the orders); fully automatic is still being tested — meme sniping is too risky, and most of them go to zero.
  • Elder Tang: AI is mediocre at technical analysis; its fundamental-analysis ability should in theory be stronger than its technical analysis, but in practice much is still missed at the decision end, and ordinary people's decision-making will not improve smoothly because of it. What is truly meaningful is a personal AI trained by a mature trading system and high-win-rate traders — "if Jiaozhu builds a well-trained AI, I would definitely pay for it".
  • Michael: AI cannot create strategies; it can only execute strategies and help him with risk control — raising the probability of not blowing up, and strictly executing take-profit and stop-loss, because "people are greedy": for example, when price falls to 75,000 you want to wait for 74,000 to enter, whereas the AI has already acted by 79999999 (referring to the take-profit level).
  • Jiaozhu: she has been working on her own strategy since April this year and has posted signal strategies on Twitter, but they are only signals and cannot run by themselves; she is working on automatic signal execution. Manual trading is affected by emotion; AI is good at quantitative arbitrage, but may miss one-sided trending moves; development will be fast — "I hope it still leaves us human traders a way to make a living".
  • DeFi Sniper's live trading (his own account): in September he ran over $50,000 through AI strategies, currently about $30,000 is running, profitable overall. He also uses AI for prediction markets: collecting news and tracking high-win-rate wallets as reference, with only 1000U invested. His project of building Jiaozhu's Chan-theory strategy into an AI was half done and unfinished — "getting one Agent truly running takes two to three months, sometimes nearly half a year"; he always keeps a manual entry point, since "not looking at it for half a year isn't realistic either".

WalletV's Quantitative Models (closing introduction by Adam)

  • Two versions prepared for beginners who can't write prompts: trend-following and momentum indicators, with a few adjustable parameters; backtested Sharpe ratio above 1.5, and a backtest over the past year's market returned about 340% (Adam's own figures; "backtests do not represent the future").

VIII. The Midterms and US Stocks: Lao Tang's Long Fundamental Essay (the most bearish voice of the session)

  • No clear pattern from midterms: many people have greatly overestimated the impact of the midterm elections — both overestimating Republican support for crypto and overestimating Democratic suppression of it; many things simply cannot be decided by a midterm election.
  • Historical statistics added by Michael (his own account): in the month after the past three midterm elections, Bitcoin fell twice and rose once, with declines of about 20% and 19% and a gain of over 30%; but over a one-year horizon all three were up, the most by about double.
  • Two reasons for the AI stock decline (Lao Tang): first, Democrats taking states like Texas where data centers are being built means environmental and regulatory requirements for AI data-center construction will tighten and construction will slow, so upstream hardware demand will be lower than previously expected; second, Anthropic's results show AI revenue growth of 116% in Q2 already dropping to 46% in Q3, and if Q4 falls to 20% it will be worse still. AI stocks had already been falling since mid-October, mainly because earnings came in far below expectations — OpenAI's earlier valuation expectation of $2–2.5 trillion has been compressed to about $1.5 trillion, and it will not IPO this year, "so there must be a problem with earnings growth"; Anthropic itself admits that AI's improvement to product-building efficiency is far below expectations.
  • The biggest black swan (a key point in Lao Tang's own words): if Democratic policies follow through, the AI-related private credit market could run into trouble, triggering a blowup in US private credit — "this bomb is very large, comparable to the 2008 subprime crisis"; US stocks would correct at least 30%, and Bitcoin reaching 40–50k would be "no surprise at all".
  • Scale of private credit (Lao Tang's own figures): during the pandemic, total US private credit was only $50 billion; it is now approaching $2 trillion, of which at least $300 billion has gone into unlisted AI infrastructure; insurance and derivatives have been built on top of this private credit, on a scale that "cannot be calculated". He had already raised this concern in a Space he hosted in October 2025.
  • His timing judgment: relatively bearish over the next 3–6 months; if US QE comes, he leans toward liquidity starting in Q2 or Q3 of next year, so the probability of an uptrend arriving is greater from Q2–Q3 of next year onward.
  • Qiege's US stock observations: on Monday (around November 3), when he and another teacher were doing a US stock column, they said "if it rebounds on Monday, run quickly; if it doesn't rebound, run anyway"; Tesla fell more than 10% that day, and memory stocks (SanDisk and the other "memory trio") were a bloodbath; the news flow was in charge, and the panic stampede was the institutions' doing. On the heat map, consumer and livelihood sectors were very resilient — he had bought Walmart over a month earlier and it was "very resilient"; short-term shorting levels were high and ideal; on time windows he had always thought October 10 might see a reversal, but only a rebound.
