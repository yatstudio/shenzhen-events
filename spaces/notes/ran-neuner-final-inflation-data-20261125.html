Ran Neuner：美联储加息前最后的通胀数据，现在是部署资金的甜点吗？

  • 主持：Ran Neuner（英文节目，单人节目，中间两次播放 Tom Lee 的采访片段）
  • 逐字稿：1134 行，覆盖 [00:00:00]–[00:56:37]

一句话

Ran 的核心判断是：PCE 3.8% 与三季度 GDP 增长 4.3% 这组"火上浇油"的数据，意味着美联储 12 月几乎铁定加息、市场正进入一段加息周期；但这轮加息是从 4% 起步、美国背着 40 万亿美元债务、付不起长期高利率，所以他不认为会是一轮漫长的加息——纳指已跌 20%，这正是他说的"开始部署资金的甜点"：现在买会难受几个月，不买六个月后回头看会觉得"明明那么明显"。

一、数据：火上浇油

  • PCE 通胀（美联储最看重的通胀指标，不是 CPI）环比上升 0.3%，符合预期；同比 3.8%，美联储目标是 2%。
  • 三季度美国 GDP 增长 4.3%，远高于预期的 3.4%——经济增速比预期快约 30%。
  • 他的比喻：通胀 3.8% 是火，经济增长极快是往火上浇油；美联储认为通胀是因为增长太快，要压通胀就得压增长，办法就是加息。
  • 12 月加息概率 97.8%，1 月再加息的概率 58.4%；核心 PCE 从 9 月的 3.1% 加速到 10 月的 3.3%，强化了通胀顽固的担忧。
  • PCE 的最大推手是汽油：10 月测算期油价在 106–120 美元之间（中期选举前的高点），现在已回落到 92 美元。
  • 市场反应：纳指期货跌 3.27%（盘中一度跌 4.2%），创日内新低；BTC 也在日内低点附近，正往周末低点 55,000 美元回落；标普 500 跌 2.69%；英伟达回到 142 美元，黄仁勋回归的涨幅已全部回吐。

二、加息周期：市场讨厌的是快加息，不是加息本身

  • 市场不喜欢加息：加息让美元（DXY）走强，所有以美元计价的资产走弱。
  • 慢而有序的加息可以接受：2015 年 11 月到 2019 年，美联储用了四年加息，市场先小跌、之后一路上涨。
  • 市场真正讨厌的是快速加息，如 2021–2022 年：美联储一宣布加息，市场就开始抢跑下跌；2022 年 1 月到 10 月（273 天）市场一路下跌，纳指从宣布加息到见底跌了 38%；市场在加息周期结束前就见底了——2022 年 10 月市场开始恢复，而利率直到 2023 年 7 月才见顶。
  • 规律：市场从加息消息一出就开始跌，一直跌到加息周期过半（约 50–60%）才转向；所以任务是预估加息会持续多久——市场一嗅到美联储要停手、要暂停，就会开始上涨。

三、AI 交易是靠债务融资撑起来的

  • 他引用 Bill Ackman 的推文：美联储 12 月 9 日再犯错；金融环境已大幅收紧；SpaceX 这家投资级公司刚以 8.25% 的利率发有担保债，而 IPO 时无担保债利率不到 6%——AI 公司的借贷成本上升了约 2.5 个百分点，融资利率涨了 30% 以上。
  • 整个经济由 AI 革命驱动，整个 AI 革命由债务融资驱动；加息等于给整个 AI 扩张拉手刹。
  • 当天个股：英伟达 142 美元、SpaceX 回到 115 美元、CoreWeave 跌到 37 美元的历史低点；唯一逆势的市场是印度市场，原因是它不沾 AI；沃尔玛接近历史高点，同样因为不沾 AI。
  • 如果利率继续上升，数据中心项目建不起来，甚至可能还不上债；通胀不减速、增长在加速，美联储要把通胀压回 2%，就得一直加下去。

四、软银：借了 500 多亿美元投 AI，股价从高点跌 70%

  • 软银从高点跌了 70%，回到 2024 年 7 月的水平；原因是这家公司在 12 个月里借了 500 多亿美元投 AI 项目（主要是 OpenAI 这类）。
  • 孙正义（口播 "Masha-San"/"Mashayosi Sun"）是出了名的 all-in 型投资者，以前也有过 50% 的大回撤；不沾 AI 交易的公司则在涨。

五、两种情景：短暂修正，还是 12 个月熊市

  • 短暂修正的例子：2025 年纳指跌 26%，只用了 50 天就跌完、然后恢复。
  • 长期修正的例子：2021–2022 年加息周期那次，约一年。
  • 现在纳指已跌 20%，BTC 跌破 57,500 美元，Zcash 跌破 700 美元；市场开始认为这会是一场持久修正。
  • 情景一（反弹）：为反弹做好准备；情景二（长期熊市）：如果进入像 2021–2022 年那样的 12 个月纳指熊市，就完全脱离四年周期论，"比特币在中期选举后见底"就成了空想；但如果市场某时看到美联储转向、纳指恢复，比特币也可能快速恢复。
  • 他认为值得认真讨论的正是没人敢说的第二种情景。

六、Tom Lee 的另一面：现在就买

  • 节目中两次播放 Tom Lee 的采访片段："你会买这次抛售吗？会。我认为我们已接近抛售的尾声，因为做空科技已成为共识交易；收益率已经回落；美联储之前很鹰派，现在可能转向鸽派；利率曾触及 5%、人们担心到 6%，现在已回到 5% 以下。"
  • Tom Lee 看好：AI 与存储瓶颈、Mag 7 正在见底、软件、加密、广泛的周期股与金融股；他称之为"压缩的弹簧"——基本面还在。
  • 历史类比：1997 年，公开互联网与浏览器推出三年后，思科在格林斯潘"非理性繁荣"言论后大跌 50%，一年后又涨回 18 美元（口播数字，按其原话记录）；ChatGPT 刚过三周年，AI 交易还很早期。

七、这轮加息不一样：从 4% 起步，付不起长期高利率

  • 此前两次加息周期都是从接近零的利率起步（2015 年、2020 年新冠后降息至零并维持太久，鲍威尔因此被称为 "Too Late Jerome Powell"）；而这轮是从联邦基金利率约 4–4.25% 起步。
  • 美国国债 40 万亿美元，按约 4% 的利率，每年利息支出 1.6 万亿美元，约占 GDP 的 4%；再往上加，整个国家会被压垮。
  • 因此他的论点：这不会是一轮漫长的激进加息；美联储只是从略低于 4% 微调到略高于 4%，不会加到 8% 或 9%，否则就是回到 1970 年代、修正持续数年。
  • 另一组数据：Trueflation 显示美国真实通胀为 2.98% 且在下降；如果油价继续回落，通胀可能到 2.6–2.7%；他的情景是：1 月民主党上台、战争结束、油价下来，2–3 月通胀开始回落，AI 交易随之反转。
  • 结论：加息周期不会永远持续，因为它是从 5%（口播，约数）这个高位起步的，"除非你要加到 6%、7%、8%、9%、10%，那世界就有比这大得多的问题了"。

八、买还是等：纳指 200 周均线与他的"甜点"理论

  • 前提假设：比特币与股市的相关性处于多年来最高点，股市恢复、比特币才恢复。
  • 纳指 200 周均线：除 2008–2009 年金融危机与 2022–2023 年加息周期短暂跌破（仅跌破 6%）外，纳指一般都守在 200 周均线之上；历次修正大多在 200 周均线附近止住。
  • 两个选择：现在买（纳指已从历史高点跌 20%），或等 200 周均线（距历史高点 33%，还需再跌约 16–17%）；问题是如果等、而它永远到不了，你会后悔。
  • 他的推文原话："如果你现在部署，准备好难受一阵子、觉得自己买早了、犯了大错；如果你现在不部署，准备好难受——因为你心里清楚，纳指 20% 的修正本来就足够值得开始部署。"
  • "甜点"就是这个位置：你明知买了还会继续跌、会难受，但你也知道如果不买，六个月后回头看会觉得"明明那么明显"；他认为 AI 革命依然完整——戴尔财报虽然暴雷，但报告了存储器严重短缺，说明数据中心与 AI 的需求远大于供给，基本面很强。
  • 如果买错了：如果市场再跌 16% 然后恢复，2025 年 2 月那次修正不到四个月就收复了失地；在半山腰部署的话，两个月就回本；最坏情况（2021–2022 年式长期熊市）：在标普跌 20% 时部署，要被套一年才见钱；但那次利率是从 0 加到 5%，现在完全不是那个起点。

九、回测数据：纳指跌 20% 时买，比等 200 周均线更划算

  • QQQ（纳指）回测：历史上触及 200 周均线 3 次、接近但没碰到 5 次——等触及才买的话，有 5 次会错过入场；触及后买入，新高分别在 30 周、16 周内出现。
  • 投入 10,000 美元、纳指跌 20% 时买入并持有一年：典型回报 +38%，最好情况 +83%；持有两年：典型 +60%，最好 +100%；最坏情况是进一步回撤、但持有后收复。
  • 等 200 周均线再买：持有两年典型回报只有 +15%，最好 +80%——结论是跌 20% 时部署比等 200 周均线更划算。
  • 半导体指数 SOX：今年涨幅已全部回吐（跌 45.3%，回到 1 月水平）；每次跌 40% 时买入，持有 1 年典型回报 +42%，最好 +132%；持有 10 年典型回报翻倍；2021–2022 年加息周期中 SOX 跌了约 47%，现在已从高点跌 40%，200 周均线还在约 25% 之外。
  • 持有 3–4 年的话，两种买法差别不大；持有 10 年以上，等跌幅更大再买略好。他的结论：在纳指 -20% 到 200 周均线之间、SOX -40% 到 200 周均线之间买入，都是合理的。

十、情绪与比特币：恐惧指数全年第二差

  • 恐惧与贪婪指数（CoinMarketCap 口播）是全年第二差的读数（读数 8）；人们很少像现在这样对比特币与科技失去信心，连最坚定的信仰者也是如此。
  • Vinny Lingham（把 Ran 带进加密圈的人）发问："帮我理解一下比特币未来五年的牛市理由——比特币现在的价格比五年前还低，而这期间政府印了钱、通胀不再是暂时的、比特币硬顶 2100 万、终极抗通胀资产……下一个催化剂是什么？"
  • Ran 的回答：每次找催化剂都找不到，不找的时候催化剂反而来了；有评论说 Saylor 就是催化剂——他的看法是 Saylor 回购了 28% 的 STRC、稀释了 MSTR 股东，MSTR 的 MNAV（净资产溢价倍数）是历史最低，"这家伙做的事简直是狗屎"，以后会专门做一期 STRC。
  • 最后的市场检查：BTC 回到 58,000 美元，纳指仍跌 4%；一年前部署的投资者没赚到钱，两年前部署的投资者在纳指上年化赚了 16.8%。"是时候开始部署了，我不知道还能怎么跟你们说。"

十一、他的自我检讨："过早部署症"（premature deployation）

  • 定义：在修正初期部署太多，越往下跌部署得越少——这正是他本轮犯的错。
  • 他手上有 700 万美元可部署，已部署约 50%，还剩 330 万美元；问题是修正初期他就激进部署了，本应一开始少部署、越害怕越要多部署；现在越跌越害怕、部署得越少，"应该反过来——价格越便宜，越要多部署"。
  • 他说这会是本轮学到的教训，以后就用 "premature deployation" 这个词来概括。

十二、AI 革命为什么还会继续

  • 他公司的会计 Matt：以前分析准备一家公司的财务报表要一个月，现在用 Claude 三到八分钟就能出完整分析报告；开发 Bubbles 应用花了几个月，而同一个开发者用 AI 一周就搭出了一个交易终端。
  • "AI 让我们的生产力提高了百万倍……那不会改变，只会指数级变快"；不会因为市场下跌就回去用搜索引擎、回去人工读财报；还需要存储、需要技术、需要数据中心；"宁愿多付一点数据中心费用、不雇 10 个会计，30 秒拿到答案，而不是等 10 天。"
  • SOX 指数自 2000 年以来的图表一直在向上，因为人们越来越依赖技术、越来越依赖半导体；"我们正处在技术革命的开端，每个人都在用 AI，不会倒退。"

十三、节目尾段：Martin 的新节目与黑 Friday 推广

  • Martin（曾"劫持"过 Ran 的节目）回来宣传他的新节目 "247 Crypto Live"：《我是如何找到底部》，用 2022 年找到比特币底部的三张图，找类似的形态，认为现在接近积累区间、信号接近触发。
  • Ran 推广自己的 247 研究小组：10 多名研究员、用专有 AI 工具每天全天扫描市场；Black Friday 特价：4,000 人规模、还剩 138 个名额，每月不到 100 美元（原价 200 美元），注册送最高 15 万美元的 funded account。

  • 整個經濟由 AI 革命驅動，整個 AI 革命由債務融資驅動；升息等於給整個 AI 擴張拉手煞車。
  • 當天個股：輝達 142 美元、SpaceX 回到 115 美元、CoreWeave 跌到 37 美元的歷史低點；唯一逆勢的市場是印度市場，原因是它不沾 AI；沃爾瑪接近歷史高點，同樣因為不沾 AI。
  • 如果利率繼續上升，資料中心專案建不起來，甚至可能還不上債；通膨不減速、成長在加速，美聯儲要把通膨壓回 2%，就得一直加下去。

  • 孫正義（口播 "Masha-San"/"Mashayosi Sun"）是出了名的 all-in 型投資者，以前也有過 50% 的大回撤；不沾 AI 交易的公司則在漲。

五、兩種情景：短暫修正，還是 12 個月熊市

  • 短暫修正的例子：2025 年納指跌 26%，只用了 50 天就跌完、然後恢復。
  • 長期修正的例子：2021–2022 年升息週期那次，約一年。
  • 現在納指已跌 20%，BTC 跌破 57,500 美元，Zcash 跌破 700 美元；市場開始認為這會是一場持久修正。
  • 情景一（反彈）：為反彈做好準備；情景二（長期熊市）：如果進入像 2021–2022 年那樣的 12 個月納指熊市，就完全脫離四年週期論，「比特幣在期中選舉後見底」就成了空想；但如果市場某時看到美聯儲轉向、納指恢復，比特幣也可能快速恢復。
  • 他認為值得認真討論的正是沒人敢說的第二種情景。

六、Tom Lee 的另一面：現在就買

  • 節目中兩次播放 Tom Lee 的採訪片段：「你會買這次拋售嗎？會。我認為我們已接近拋售的尾聲，因為做空科技已成為共識交易；殖利率已經回落；美聯儲之前很鷹派，現在可能轉向鴿派；利率曾觸及 5%、人們擔心到 6%，現在已回到 5% 以下。」
  • Tom Lee 看好：AI 與儲存瓶頸、Mag 7 正在見底、軟體、加密、廣泛的週期股與金融股；他稱之為「壓縮的彈簧」——基本面還在。

七、這輪升息不一樣：從 4% 起步，付不起長期高利率

  • 此前兩次升息週期都是從接近零的利率起步（2015 年、2020 年新冠後降息至零並維持太久，鮑爾因此被稱為 "Too Late Jerome Powell"）；而這輪是從聯邦基金利率約 4–4.25% 起步。
  • 美國國債 40 萬億美元，按約 4% 的利率，每年利息支出 1.6 萬億美元，約佔 GDP 的 4%；再往上加，整個國家會被壓垮。
  • 因此他的論點：這不會是一輪漫長的激進升息；美聯儲只是從略低於 4% 微調到略高於 4%，不會加到 8% 或 9%，否則就是回到 1970 年代、修正持續數年。
  • 另一組數據：Trueflation 顯示美國真實通膨為 2.98% 且在下降；如果油價繼續回落，通膨可能到 2.6–2.7%；他的情景是：1 月民主黨上台、戰爭結束、油價下來，2–3 月通膨開始回落，AI 交易隨之反轉。
  • 結論：升息週期不會永遠持續，因為它是從 5%（口播，約數）這個高位起步的，「除非你要加到 6%、7%、8%、9%、10%，那世界就有比這大得多的問題了」。

八、買還是等：納指 200 週均線與他的「甜點」理論

  • 前提假設：比特幣與股市的相關性處於多年來最高點，股市恢復、比特幣才恢復。

  • 兩個選擇：現在買（納指已從歷史高點跌 20%），或等 200 週均線（距歷史高點 33%，還需再跌約 16–17%）；問題是如果等、而它永遠到不了，你會後悔。
  • 他的推文原話：「如果你現在部署，準備好難受一陣子、覺得自己買早了、犯了大錯；如果你現在不部署，準備好難受——因為你心裡清楚，納指 20% 的修正本來就足夠值得開始部署。」
  • 「甜點」就是這個位置：你明知買了還會繼續跌、會難受，但你也知道如果不買，六個月後回頭看會覺得「明明那麼明顯」；他認為 AI 革命依然完整——戴爾財報雖然暴雷，但報告了記憶體嚴重短缺，說明資料中心與 AI 的需求遠大於供給，基本面很強。
  • 如果買錯了：如果市場再跌 16% 然後恢復，2025 年 2 月那次修正不到四個月就收復了失地；在半山腰部署的話，兩個月就回本；最壞情況（2021–2022 年式長期熊市）：在標普跌 20% 時部署，要被套一年才見錢；但那次利率是從 0 加到 5%，現在完全不是那個起點。

Ran Neuner: The Final Inflation Data Before the Fed's Rate Hike — Is Now the Sweet Spot to Deploy Capital?

  • Broadcast: https://x.com/i/broadcasts/2qVYJBnbmnyMrM
  • Time: Aired 2026-11-25 at 22:35 CST, total length about 56 min 57 sec (3416.79 seconds)
  • Host: Ran Neuner (English-language program, solo show, with two clips of a Tom Lee interview played in between)
  • Transcript: 1,134 lines, covering [00:00:00]–[00:56:37]
  • Note: Everything below is the speaker's own stated views, unverified. See the end for ASR normalizations.

In One Sentence

Ran's core judgment: the combination of PCE at 3.8% and Q3 GDP growth of 4.3% — "fuel on the fire" — means the Fed is almost certain to hike in December and markets are entering a rate-hiking period. But because this hiking cycle starts from 4%, with the US carrying $40 trillion of debt and unable to afford high rates for long, he does not think the hikes will be prolonged. With the Nasdaq already down 20%, this is what he calls the "sweet spot to start deploying capital": buy now and you will feel sick for a few months; don't buy, and six months from now you will look back and say it was "so obvious."

1. The Data: Fuel on the Fire

  • PCE inflation (the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, not CPI) rose 0.3% month on month, in line with expectations; year on year it is 3.8%, against the Fed's 2% target.
  • US GDP grew 4.3% in Q3, far above the 3.4% expected — the economy grew about 30% faster than anticipated.
  • His analogy: 3.8% inflation is the fire, and very fast economic growth is fuel on that fire. The Fed believes inflation is happening because growth is too fast; to slow inflation it must slow growth, and the way to do that is to raise rates.
  • The probability of a December hike is 97.8%, and of another hike in January 58.4%; core PCE accelerated from 3.1% in September to 3.3% in October, reinforcing concerns about persistent inflation.
  • The biggest culprit in PCE was gasoline: during the October measurement period oil prices were between $106 and $120 (the highs before the midterm elections); oil has since fallen back to $92.
  • Market reaction: Nasdaq futures fell 3.27% (down 4.2% at one point intraday), hitting session lows; BTC was also near its session lows, tracking back toward the weekend low of $55,000; the S&P 500 fell 2.69%; Nvidia was back at $142, having given back all of the gains from Jensen Huang's return.

2. The Hiking Cycle: What Markets Hate Is Fast Hikes, Not Hikes Themselves

  • Markets don't like rate hikes: hikes strengthen the dollar (DXY), and every asset denominated in dollars gets weaker.
  • Slow, orderly hikes are acceptable: from November 2015 to 2019 the Fed raised rates over four years; markets dipped slightly at first and then kept going up.
  • What markets truly hate is fast hikes, like 2021–2022: as soon as the Fed announced hikes, markets front-ran them and fell; from January to October 2022 (273 days) markets went down, with the Nasdaq falling 38% from the hike announcement to the bottom. Markets bottomed before the hiking cycle ended — markets began recovering in October 2022, while rates only topped out in July 2023.
  • The pattern: markets start falling the moment hike talk begins, and keep falling until roughly halfway through the hiking cycle (about 50–60%) before turning. So the job is to estimate how long the hikes will last — the moment markets sniff that the Fed is going to stop or pause, they will start going up again.

3. The AI Trade Is Propped Up by Debt Financing

  • He cited a tweet by Bill Ackman: the Fed is making another mistake on December 9; financial conditions have tightened massively; SpaceX, an investment-grade company, just issued secured debt at 8.25%, versus below 6% for its unsecured bonds at IPO — AI companies' borrowing costs have risen by about 2.5 percentage points, a financing-rate increase of over 30%.
  • The entire economy is powered by the AI revolution, and the entire AI revolution is powered by debt financing; raising rates is effectively pulling the handbrake on the whole AI expansion.
  • Stocks that day: Nvidia at $142, SpaceX back at $115, CoreWeave at its all-time low of $37; the only market bucking the trend was India, because it is not exposed to AI; Walmart was near all-time highs for the same reason — no AI exposure.
  • If rates keep rising, data center projects can't get built and may not even be able to make their debt repayments; inflation isn't slowing and growth is accelerating, so if the Fed wants inflation back to 2%, it will have to keep hiking.

4. SoftBank: Borrowed Over $50 Billion to Bet on AI, Stock Down 70% From Its Highs

  • SoftBank is down 70% from its highs, back to its July 2024 level, because the company borrowed more than $50 billion over 12 months to invest in AI projects (mainly the likes of OpenAI).
  • Masayoshi Son (spoken as "Masha-San"/"Mashayosi Sun") is a famously all-in investor who has had 50% drawdowns before; companies with no exposure to the AI trade are going up instead.

5. Two Scenarios: A Short Correction, or a 12-Month Bear Market

  • Example of a short correction: in 2025 the Nasdaq fell 26% in just 50 days, then recovered.
  • Example of a long correction: the 2021–2022 hiking cycle, which lasted about a year.
  • Now the Nasdaq is down 20%, BTC has broken below $57,500, and Zcash is under $700; markets are starting to price in a prolonged correction.
  • Scenario one (the bounce): be ready for the bounce. Scenario two (a prolonged bear market): if we enter a 12-month Nasdaq bear market like 2021–2022, it breaks completely away from the four-year cycle thesis, and "Bitcoin bottoming just after the midterms" becomes a pipe dream. But if at some point markets see the Fed turn and the Nasdaq recovers, Bitcoin could recover quickly too.
  • The scenario he thinks is worth seriously discussing is the second one — the one nobody has the guts to talk about.

6. Tom Lee's Other Side: Buy Now

  • The show twice played clips of a Tom Lee interview: "Would you buy the sell-off yet? Yes. I think we're near the end stages of the sell-off, because it's become a consensus short to be shorting tech. Yields, which were really worrying markets, have backed away. The Fed was quite hawkish, now it's flipping potentially dovish. Rates were touching 5%, people fearing 6%, and are now back below 5%."
  • Tom Lee is bullish on: AI and the memory bottleneck, the Mag 7 bottoming, software, crypto, and the broad cyclical trade including financials; he calls it a "coiled spring" — the fundamentals are holding in.
  • Historical analogy: in 1997, three years after the launch of the public internet and browsers, Cisco had a massive 50% drawdown after Greenspan's "irrational exuberance" remarks, and a year later was back at $18 (spoken figures, recorded as he stated them); ChatGPT has just crossed its third anniversary, and the AI trade is still very early.

7. This Hiking Cycle Is Different: It Starts From 4%, and Long-Term High Rates Are Unaffordable

  • The previous two hiking cycles both started from rates very close to zero (2015; and 2020, when rates were cut to zero after COVID and kept there too long — which is why Powell was called "Too Late Jerome Powell"). This cycle starts from a federal funds rate of about 4–4.25%.
  • The US has $40 trillion in debt; at roughly 4% interest, annual interest payments are $1.6 trillion, about 4% of GDP. Push rates much higher and you cripple the entire country.
  • His thesis, therefore: this will not be a prolonged, aggressive hiking cycle. The Fed is only making slight adjustments, from just under 4% to just over 4%; it will not go to 8% or 9%, because that would take us back to the 1970s, a correction lasting years.
  • Another data point: Truflation shows US inflation at 2.98% and decreasing; if oil prices keep dropping, inflation could reach 2.6–2.7%. His scenario: in January the Democrats come in, the war ends, oil comes down, and by February–March inflation starts coming down and the AI trade turns.
  • Conclusion: the hiking cycle will not go on forever, because it is starting from the high base of 5% (spoken, approximate) — "unless you're going to go to 6%, 7%, 8%, 9%, 10%, and if we get there, the world has much bigger problems than the ones we're talking about."

8. Buy or Wait: The Nasdaq's 200-Week Moving Average and His "Sweet Spot" Theory

  • Working assumption: Bitcoin's correlation with stock markets is at its highest in many years; when stock markets recover, Bitcoin will recover, and until then it won't.
  • The Nasdaq's 200-week moving average: except for the 2008–2009 financial crisis and a brief dip in 2022–23 (only 6% below), the Nasdaq generally stays above its 200-week moving average, and corrections generally stop at or around it.
  • Two choices: buy now (the Nasdaq is down 20% from its all-time highs), or wait for the 200-week moving average (33% below the highs, another 16–17% down). The problem: if you wait and it never gets there, you'll kick yourself.
  • His tweet, verbatim: "If you're deploying now, prepare to feel sick for a while thinking you were too early and you made a terrible mistake. If you're not deploying now, prepare to feel sick for not deploying when you knew in your heart of hearts that a 20% correction in the Nasdaq was a good enough correction to actually start deploying."
  • The "sweet spot" is exactly this point: you know that if you deploy now you will feel sick because prices keep going down, but you also know that if you don't deploy, in six months you'll look back and say it was so obvious. He believes the AI revolution is still firmly intact — Dell's earnings crashed, but Dell reported a massive shortage of memory, meaning demand from data centers and the AI revolution far exceeds supply; the fundamentals are strong.
  • If you buy and you're wrong: if the market drops another 16% and then recovers — the February 2025 correction took less than four months to recover to the same levels, and deploying halfway down would have meant two months to get your money back. In the worst case (a 2021–2022-style long bear market), deploying when the S&P was down 20% meant being wrong for a year before seeing money. But back then rates went from zero to 5%; we are nowhere near zero rates now.

9. Backtest Data: Buying at Nasdaq -20% Beats Waiting for the 200-Week Moving Average

  • QQQ (Nasdaq) backtest: historically there have been three touches of the 200-week moving average and five near-misses — if you had waited for the touch, you would have missed the entry five times. After touches, new highs came within 30 weeks and 16 weeks respectively.
