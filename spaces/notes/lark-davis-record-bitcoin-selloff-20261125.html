Lark Davis：比特币 ETF 创纪录抛售，特朗普关税又回来了

一句话

Lark 的判断是：11 月比特币 ETF 流出创历史纪录、稳定币每天流出 4 亿美元、期权交易员极度看空，但链上投降指标也到了过去每次熊市底部的位置——"要么比特币就此死掉，要么我们正接近一个难以置信的机会"，而量子威胁是机构资金迟迟不回来的最后一块绊脚石。

一、关税又回来了

美国最早本周重新对中国、欧盟和另外 14 个国家加征关税；欧盟、日本、韩国可能面临 15% 或更低的税率，印度、越南可能更高，中国预计面临更高的税率；新关税可能覆盖大多数进口商品（钢铁、铝、汽车已有单独关税）；如果各国不解决工业产能过剩问题，税率还可以进一步上调。

Lark 的调侃：特朗普上周才和习近平握手，回到家就"加他关税，狠狠地加"。他怀疑这是否只是虚张声势、吓唬市场好低价买回来——毕竟最高法院之前已经否决过关税（他提到已有 60 亿到 3280 亿美元正退还给进口商，数字口播不清），纳斯达克从 2025 年 4 月低点已涨 53%。

二、比特币 ETF：史上最大流出月

  • BTC 现价 58,500 美元：前几天从 55,000 美元反弹，3 天内几乎涨了 1 万美元（黄仁勋回归那波拉升），现在已基本全部回吐；再测 55,000 美元一点也不会让他吃惊。
  • 11 月 ETF 流出 47–49 亿美元（SoVali 统计 49.4 亿，其他统计 47.1 亿），已超过 6 月的 45 亿美元——比特币 ETF 历史上最大的单月流出，本月还剩 3 个交易日，很可能突破 50 亿。
  • 他说 ETF 现在有个大问题：上涨时每天 10 亿美元涌入是利好，现在是 ETF 把巨量筹码砸进一个极其单薄、没人敢买的市场；市场只能吸收有限的抛压，价格随之下跌。ETF 净资产还剩约 720 亿美元可抛（他的玩笑说法）。
  • iBit 平均买入成本 82,000 美元，买家深套；"游客买顶卖底"。Coinbase 溢价指数已跌到 Luna/FTX 崩盘以来最低水平。
  • 稳定币自 10 月 1 日以来每天流出 4 亿美元，累计约 300 亿美元；加上 ETF 每天约 5 亿美元、ETH ETF 每天约 1 亿美元流出，"所有人都在卖一切"。
  • 期权交易员极度看空：涌入看跌期权的速度是 2022 年投降以来最快，看跌/看涨持仓比为 2021 年以来最高。
  • 本轮熊市已持续 411 天以上，是比特币历史上最长的一次，但（目前为止）也是最浅的一次；上一轮从顶到底用了 378 天，此前最长纪录是 410 天。

三、投降指标 vs 底部信号：两边都有

投降证据： - CryptoQuant：比特币投资者水下程度已到历史上每次熊市底部的水平；每轮熊市都会跌破投资者平均成本价——按 Glassnode 数据（口播"doodles"）平均成本约 53,250 美元；如果走 2022 年那种跌法，目标是 40,000 美元，3 万多、4 万出头的目标"近得让人不舒服"。 - 已实现亏损（相对过去一年流入资金）为 FTX 以来最重，一周内几乎翻倍：大量资金在 75,000–85,000 美元区间流入 BTC，现在都在认亏离场。 - 矿工在卖、数字资产财库公司（DAT）在卖、ETF 在卖、巨鲸在卖，"所有人都在卖"。 - 今年已有 3–4 家加密货币交易所破产关门（他点名 BitMart、AscendEX），DAT 公司把比特币还给股东，稳定币 6 周流出 300 亿美元。

底部信号： - "比特币要么死掉，要么我们正接近一个难以置信的机会"——一个 4 年半没出现过的链上信号正在出现。 - BTC 相对长期趋势的便宜程度，在整个价格史上只有 60 天达到过（底部 1%）；Charles Edwards 说 BTC 自 2022 年 11 月以来没这么便宜过，链上指标看起来很强，问题是资金流太差。 - 按四年周期理论，底部应该就在上周六或未来几天内——2017 年那轮从顶到底基本正好一年，本轮也在这个窗口里。

他的综合判断： 大部分投降可能已经发生了（矿工、DAT 的抛售大头已过），但还有"时间型投降"的风险：ETF 持有者拿了 18 个月–2 年，看着英伟达、美光、闪迪的涨幅，会想"我的 BTC ETF 跌了 50%，美光可没跌 50%，卖 BTC 买美光上行空间可能更大"。在真正的买盘回来之前，价格仍可能更低；按 Charles Edwards 的说法，量子威胁的路线图明确下来，可能会把大资金带回来。

五、访谈：Isabel Foxen Duke 谈比特币量子威胁与 BIP 360

威胁是什么： 比特币钱包地址由公私钥密码学保护，具体是椭圆曲线密码学，被认为"量子不安全"——足够强的量子计算机可以用 Shor 算法破解它，从公钥推导出私钥、偷走资金。

谁最危险： 最危险的是老式地址——中本聪的约 22,000 个钱包（100 多万枚 BTC），公钥直接写在地址里；Taproot 地址同样把公钥暴露在地址中。大多数现代地址的公钥受哈希保护，哈希被认为相当抗量子，所以没花过的地址在静态时基本是量子安全的；一旦你从某个地址花过钱，花费过程就会暴露公钥，该地址随即变得量子不安全。钱包卫生：不要从同一个地址花钱后又往回打钱。

但个人防护够吗： 她认为不够——如果约 600 万枚 BTC 量子不安全、一下子"没了"，你手里的 BTC 还值钱吗？需要整体解决方案，而不只是想着自己的密钥。

时间线： 谷歌内部模型目标是 2029 年底前升级完所有系统；以太坊方面在推 2028 年；比特币社区讨论过 2032 年目标。对普通人来说，未来几年的实际量子风险还很低，不必太担心；但从投资角度，机构在等一个解决方案出来之前可能会先退后一步。

开发者能不能做到： 她认为人们低估了开发者——过去开发者迟迟没有大动作，是因为那些都是"有了更好"的东西，鉴于比特币的重要性与涉及的资金，大家对改动比特币非常保守；量子抗性是"必须有"的东西，开发者社区对此已有共识，只剩下细节：引入哪种签名、怎么引入。她预计 2029 年之前比特币共识层就可能出现完整的抗量子输出类型/地址类型加抗量子签名；难的是让所有人都迁移过去——最耗时的不是升级本身，而是让成千上万的人把币挪走，别等到 Q-Day 前三天才匆忙迁移。

中本聪的币怎么办： 她认为有两种走法。第一种是"难走的路"：达不成共识，出现一堆分叉、市场选赢家（像当年的硬分叉战争）；大多数市场参与者会选让自己 BTC 最值钱的那条链，也就是不让黑客把 600 万枚币砸向市场的那条。第二种是技术回收方案：先冻结这些币，再用零知识技术让持有人用助记词（而非私钥）证明所有权来取回；闪电网络发明者 Tadge Dryja（口播"Taz Drijah"）正在全职开发这类恢复协议。如果回收技术足够好，"先冻结、临时冻结、能用其他方式证明所有权"就很容易说服大家；中本聪本人可能永远取不回，但那些只是忘了迁移的普通持币人不会被坑。

BIP 360 是什么： 让比特币抗量子需要两样东西——抗量子地址类型（输出类型）和抗量子签名。BIP 360 引入的是前者：一种名为"pay-to-Merkle-root"（P2MR）的输出类型，基本是 pay-to-taproot 的翻版，但去掉了 Taproot 中量子脆弱的部分；它是目前唯一正式规范化的抗量子输出类型提案，处于领先位置。签名方面领跑的是一个哈希签名方案，口播为"Shrinks"（名称按 ASR 保留存疑），由 Blockstream 研究总监 Jonas Nick 及合作者开发；她对未来几年内拿到输出类型和签名方案"相当看好"。

BIP 360 的争议： 一开始就禁用量子脆弱部分，会失去 Taproot 的一些功能——普通持有者用不上，但做 L2 的开发者很喜欢。最大的竞争提案是：保留这些好用但量子脆弱的 Taproot 功能，并约定一个未来日期，等量子计算机临近时再禁用椭圆曲线密码学；难题是没人知道量子计算机什么时候来，"禁用日期定在哪一天"本身就有风险。

升级本身的风险： 这是极其敏感、重要的代码，绝不能操之过急引入 bug，尤其是在 LLM 时代 bug 更容易被发现和利用；开发者采取的姿态是对的：别磨蹭、认真对待威胁、尽快在负责任的前提下给用户抗量子选项，但不要 rushing；Dan Boneh（口播"Dan Benet"）也谈过这种节奏的重要性。

谁在协作： 比特币安全联盟（Bitcoin Security Consortium）——不到一年前，大机构持币者（Fidelity、贝莱德、MicroStrategy 等）聚在一起，承诺出资确保量子问题得到解决，资金主要以捐赠形式流向已有的比特币开发机构（Chaincode Labs、Brink、Jack Dorsey 的 Block 旗下 Spiral）。MARA 比较特殊：早在"比特币第二季"L2 热潮时就在研发层面探索抗量子侧链，CEO Fred Thiel 很早就重视这个问题；她本人曾做 Ordinals 协议与 L2，MARA 聘她与另一位 BIP 360 联合提案人 Hunter Beast 一起做这条侧链，Hunter 已起草 BIP 360，她参与了 V2 的撰写；MARA 由此有了两名员工共同提案了第一个抗量子 BIP。

路线图什么时候来： 比特币真正去中心化，没有哪个管理机构会发布路线图；但各家开发机构（Chaincode Labs、Brink、Blockstream 等）会陆续公开各自的内部路线图。Lark 约她六个月后再来更新进展。

一、關稅又回來了

美國最早本週重新對中國、歐盟和另外 14 個國家加徵關稅；歐盟、日本、韓國可能面臨 15% 或更低的稅率，印度、越南可能更高，中國預計面臨更高的稅率；新關稅可能覆蓋大多數進口商品（鋼鐵、鋁、汽車已有單獨關稅）；如果各國不解決工業產能過剩問題，稅率還可以進一步上調。

Lark 的調侃：川普上週才和習近平握手，回到家就「加他關稅，狠狠地加」。他懷疑這是否只是虛張聲勢、嚇唬市場好低價買回來——畢竟最高法院之前已經否決過關稅（他提到已有 60 億到 3280 億美元正退還給進口商，數字口播不清），納斯達克從 2025 年 4 月低點已漲 53%。

五、訪談：Isabel Foxen Duke 談比特幣量子威脅與 BIP 360

威脅是什麼： 比特幣錢包地址由公私鑰密碼學保護，具體是橢圓曲線密碼學，被認為「量子不安全」——足夠強的量子電腦可以用 Shor 算法破解它，從公鑰推導出私鑰、偷走資金。

誰最危險： 最危險的是老式地址——中本聰的約 22,000 個錢包（100 多萬枚 BTC），公鑰直接寫在地址裡；Taproot 地址同樣把公鑰暴露在地址中。大多數現代地址的公鑰受雜湊保護，雜湊被認為相當抗量子，所以沒花過的地址在靜態時基本是量子安全的；一旦你從某個地址花過錢，花費過程就會暴露公鑰，該地址隨即變得量子不安全。錢包衛生：不要從同一個地址花錢後又往回打錢。

但個人防護夠嗎： 她認為不夠——如果約 600 萬枚 BTC 量子不安全、一下子「沒了」，你手裡的 BTC 還值錢嗎？需要整體解決方案，而不只是想著自己的密鑰。

時間線： 谷歌內部模型目標是 2029 年底前升級完所有系統；以太坊方面在推 2028 年；比特幣社群討論過 2032 年目標。對普通人來說，未來幾年的實際量子風險還很低，不必太擔心；但從投資角度，機構在等一個解決方案出來之前可能會先退後一步。

開發者能不能做到： 她認為人們低估了開發者——過去開發者遲遲沒有大動作，是因為那些都是「有了更好」的東西，鑑於比特幣的重要性與涉及的資金，大家對改動比特幣非常保守；量子抗性是「必須有」的東西，開發者社群對此已有共識，只剩下細節：引入哪種簽名、怎麼引入。她預計 2029 年之前比特幣共識層就可能出現完整的抗量子輸出類型/地址類型加抗量子簽名；難的是讓所有人都遷移過去——最耗時的不是升級本身，而是讓成千上萬的人把幣挪走，別等到 Q-Day 前三天才匆忙遷移。

中本聰的幣怎麼辦： 她認為有兩種走法。第一種是「難走的路」：達不成共識，出現一堆分叉、市場選贏家（像當年的硬分叉戰爭）；大多數市場參與者會選讓自己 BTC 最值錢的那條鏈，也就是不讓黑客把 600 萬枚幣砸向市場的那條。第二種是技術回收方案：先凍結這些幣，再用零知識技術讓持有人用助記詞（而非私鑰）證明所有權來取回；閃電網路發明者 Tadge Dryja（口播「Taz Drijah」）正在全職開發這類恢復協議。如果回收技術足夠好，「先凍結、臨時凍結、能用其他方式證明所有權」就很容易說服大家；中本聰本人可能永遠取不回，但那些只是忘了遷移的普通持幣人不會被坑。

BIP 360 是什麼： 讓比特幣抗量子需要兩樣東西——抗量子地址類型（輸出類型）和抗量子簽名。BIP 360 引入的是前者：一種名為「pay-to-Merkle-root」（P2MR）的輸出類型，基本是 pay-to-taproot 的翻版，但去掉了 Taproot 中量子脆弱的部分；它是目前唯一正式規範化的抗量子輸出類型提案，處於領先位置。簽名方面領跑的是一個雜湊簽名方案，口播為「Shrinks」（名稱按 ASR 保留存疑），由 Blockstream 研究總監 Jonas Nick 及合作者開發；她對未來幾年內拿到輸出類型和簽名方案「相當看好」。

BIP 360 的爭議： 一開始就禁用量子脆弱部分，會失去 Taproot 的一些功能——普通持有者用不上，但做 L2 的開發者很喜歡。最大的競爭提案是：保留這些好用但量子脆弱的 Taproot 功能，並約定一個未來日期，等量子電腦臨近時再禁用橢圓曲線密碼學；難題是沒人知道量子電腦什麼時候來，「禁用日期定在哪一天」本身就有風險。

升級本身的風險： 這是極其敏感、重要的程式碼，絕不能操之過急引入 bug，尤其是在 LLM 時代 bug 更容易被發現和利用；開發者採取的姿態是對的：別磨蹭、認真對待威脅、儘快在負責任的前提下給用戶抗量子選項，但不要 rushing；Dan Boneh（口播「Dan Benet」）也談過這種節奏的重要性。

誰在協作： 比特幣安全聯盟（Bitcoin Security Consortium）——不到一年前，大機構持幣者（Fidelity、貝萊德、MicroStrategy 等）聚在一起，承諾出資確保量子問題得到解決，資金主要以捐贈形式流向已有的比特幣開發機構（Chaincode Labs、Brink、Jack Dorsey 的 Block 旗下 Spiral）。MARA 比較特殊：早在「比特幣第二季」L2 熱潮時就在研發層面探索抗量子側鏈，CEO Fred Thiel 很早就重視這個問題；她本人曾做 Ordinals 協議與 L2，MARA 聘她與另一位 BIP 360 聯合提案人 Hunter Beast 一起做這條側鏈，Hunter 已起草 BIP 360，她參與了 V2 的撰寫；MARA 由此有了兩名員工共同提案了第一個抗量子 BIP。

路線圖什麼時候來： 比特幣真正去中心化，沒有哪個管理機構會發布路線圖；但各家開發機構（Chaincode Labs、Brink、Blockstream 等）會陸續公開各自的內部路線圖。Lark 約她六個月後再來更新進展。

Lark's judgment: November saw record Bitcoin ETF outflows, stablecoins bleeding $400 million a day, and options traders positioned extremely bearish — yet on-chain capitulation indicators have also reached the levels seen at every past bear-market bottom. "Either Bitcoin dies here, or we're approaching an incredible opportunity" — and the quantum threat is the last stumbling block keeping institutional money on the sidelines.

1. The tariffs are back

The US could reimpose tariffs on China, the EU and 14 other countries as early as this week; the EU, Japan and South Korea may face rates of 15% or lower, India and Vietnam possibly higher, and China is expected to face higher rates still; the new tariffs could cover most imported goods (steel, aluminum and autos already have separate tariffs); and if countries fail to address industrial overcapacity, the rates could be raised further.

2. Bitcoin ETFs: the biggest outflow month in history

  • BTC spot price $58,500: a few days ago it bounced from $55,000, rising almost $10,000 in 3 days (the Jensen Huang return rally), and has now basically given it all back; a retest of $55,000 would not surprise him at all.

  • He says ETFs now have a big problem: on the way up, $1 billion flowing in every day was bullish; now the ETFs are dumping huge amounts of supply into an extremely thin market where nobody dares to buy; the market can only absorb so much selling pressure, and the price falls accordingly. ETF net assets still have roughly $72 billion left to sell (his joking phrasing).
  • iBit's average buy-in cost is $82,000, leaving buyers deeply underwater; "tourists buy the top and sell the bottom." The Coinbase premium index has fallen to its lowest level since the Luna/FTX collapse.

Evidence of capitulation: - CryptoQuant: Bitcoin investors are as underwater as they have been at every historical bear-market bottom; every bear market breaks below investors' average cost basis — per Glassnode data (spoken as "doodles") the average cost is about $53,250; if it plays out like 2022, the target is $40,000, and targets in the low-to-mid $30,000s and low $40,000s are "uncomfortably close." - Realized losses (relative to capital inflows over the past year) are the heaviest since FTX, nearly doubling within a week: large amounts of capital flowed into BTC in the $75,000–$85,000 range and are now capitulating out at a loss. - Miners are selling, digital asset treasury companies (DATs) are selling, ETFs are selling, whales are selling — "everyone is selling." - Three to four crypto exchanges have already gone bankrupt and shut down this year (he named BitMart and AscendEX), DAT companies are returning Bitcoin to shareholders, and stablecoins saw $30 billion in outflows in 6 weeks.

Bottom signals: - "Either Bitcoin dies, or we're approaching an incredible opportunity" — an on-chain signal that hasn't appeared in four and a half years is showing up. - BTC's cheapness relative to its long-term trend has only been seen on 60 days in its entire price history (the bottom 1%); Charles Edwards says BTC hasn't been this cheap since November 2022, on-chain metrics look strong, and the problem is the terrible flows. - On four-year cycle theory, the bottom should be last Saturday or within the next few days — the 2017 cycle took almost exactly one year from top to bottom, and this cycle is in the same window.

5. Interview: Isabel Foxen Duke on Bitcoin's quantum threat and BIP 360

What the threat is: Bitcoin wallet addresses are protected by public/private key cryptography — specifically elliptic curve cryptography, which is considered "quantum unsafe": a sufficiently powerful quantum computer could break it using Shor's algorithm, deriving the private key from the public key and stealing the funds.

Who is most at risk: the most at-risk are old-style addresses — Satoshi's roughly 22,000 wallets (over 1 million BTC), where the public key is written directly into the address; Taproot addresses likewise expose the public key in the address. Most modern addresses have their public keys protected by hashing, and hashes are considered fairly quantum-resistant, so an address that has never spent is basically quantum-safe while it sits still; but once you spend from an address, the spending process exposes the public key, and that address immediately becomes quantum-unsafe. Wallet hygiene: don't send funds back into an address you have already spent from.

But is personal protection enough: she argues it isn't — if roughly 6 million BTC are quantum-unsafe and suddenly "gone," would the BTC in your hands still be worth anything? A systemic solution is needed, not just everyone minding their own keys.

Timeline: Google's internal models target upgrading all of its systems by the end of 2029; the Ethereum side is pushing for 2028; the Bitcoin community has discussed a 2032 target. For ordinary people, the practical quantum risk over the next few years is still low, so there's no need to worry much; but from an investment perspective, institutions may step back until a solution is in place.

Can the developers do it: she thinks people underestimate the developers — in the past they were slow to act because those things were "nice to have," and given Bitcoin's importance and the money involved, everyone is very conservative about changing Bitcoin; quantum resistance is a "must have," and the developer community has reached consensus on it, leaving only the details: which signature scheme to introduce, and how. She expects that by 2029 the Bitcoin consensus layer could have a complete quantum-resistant output type/address type plus quantum-resistant signatures; the hard part is getting everyone to migrate — the most time-consuming part is not the upgrade itself, but getting tens of thousands of people to move their coins instead of scrambling three days before Q-Day.

What to do about Satoshi's coins: she sees two paths. The first is "the hard road": no consensus, a pile of forks, and the market picks a winner (like the hard fork wars of the past); most market participants would choose the chain that makes their BTC worth the most — the one that doesn't let hackers dump 6 million coins onto the market. The second is a technical recovery scheme: freeze those coins first, then use zero-knowledge technology to let holders prove ownership with a seed phrase (rather than a private key) to reclaim them; Lightning Network inventor Tadge Dryja (spoken as "Taz Drijah") is working full-time on this kind of recovery protocol. If the recovery technology is good enough, "freeze first, freeze temporarily, ownership can be proven another way" becomes easy to sell to everyone; Satoshi himself may never recover his, but ordinary holders who simply forgot to migrate won't get burned.

What BIP 360 is: making Bitcoin quantum-resistant takes two things — a quantum-resistant address type (output type) and quantum-resistant signatures. BIP 360 introduces the former: an output type called "pay-to-Merkle-root" (P2MR), basically a copy of pay-to-taproot with the quantum-vulnerable parts of Taproot removed; it is currently the only formally specified quantum-resistant output type proposal, and it is in the lead. On signatures, the front-runner is a hash-based signature scheme, spoken as "Shrinks" (the name is kept as spoken, unverified), developed by Blockstream research director Jonas Nick and collaborators; she is "fairly optimistic" about having both an output type and a signature scheme within the next few years.

The controversy around BIP 360: disabling the quantum-vulnerable parts from the start means losing some Taproot functionality — ordinary holders don't use it, but L2 developers love it. The biggest competing proposal is: keep those useful but quantum-vulnerable Taproot features, and agree on a future date to disable elliptic curve cryptography when quantum computers get close; the problem is nobody knows when quantum computers will arrive, and "which day to set the disable date" is itself a risk.

The risk of the upgrade itself: this is extremely sensitive, critical code, and bugs must not be rushed in — especially in the LLM era, when bugs are easier to find and exploit; the developers' posture is the right one: don't dawdle, take the threat seriously, give users quantum-resistant options as soon as responsibly possible, but don't rush; Dan Boneh (spoken as "Dan Benet") has also spoken about the importance of this pacing.

Who is collaborating: the Bitcoin Security Consortium — less than a year ago, large institutional holders (Fidelity, BlackRock, MicroStrategy and others) came together and committed funding to make sure the quantum problem gets solved, with the money flowing mainly as donations to existing Bitcoin development organizations (Chaincode Labs, Brink, and Spiral, part of Jack Dorsey's Block). MARA is unusual: back during the "Bitcoin Season 2" L2 boom it was already exploring quantum-resistant sidechains at the R&D level, and CEO Fred Thiel took the issue seriously early on; she herself had worked on the Ordinals protocol and L2s, and MARA hired her together with fellow BIP 360 co-author Hunter Beast to build that sidechain — Hunter drafted BIP 360 and she contributed to writing V2; that gives MARA two employees who co-authored the first quantum-resistant BIP.

When is the roadmap coming: Bitcoin is truly decentralized, and no governing body will publish a roadmap; but the various development organizations (Chaincode Labs, Brink, Blockstream and others) will progressively publish their own internal roadmaps.
