会议纪要：代币化股票上线，还需要 CEX 吗？

  • 原标题：Tokenized Stocks Go Live: Does Anyone Still Need A CEX?
  • 时间：2026-10-22（周四），时长 1 小时 16 分
  • 主持：Justin Roberty，Wen Alt Season（@wenaltseason，每周四固定节目）
  • 参与：21,087 人
  • 链接：https://x.com/i/spaces/1MJgNbmvngmGL
  • 特邀嘉宾：Pop DEX（Zach Prater）；嘉宾：Daria、Katana 的 Matt/Matthew、Holder House 的 Nikita、No Nonsense Forex、Arch Lending 的 Drew、SpoutFi 的 Mark Ryan、ME Wallet 的 Dr. Jack、Piper 的 Roman
  • 说明：以下均为发言人现场自述，未经核实；英文原话为转写稿摘录，可能存在转写误差。

结论先行

  1. CEX 不会马上消失，但叙事重心已转向链上：全场没有人断言 CEX 会死，讨论的实际问题是——代币化股票和永续合约 DEX 能不能从 CEX 手里抢走交易量和用户。共识是：现在仍以加密原生用户为主，传统金融用户还没真正进来，平台现在布局是为几年后做准备。
  2. 代币化股票真正的卖点不是“能交易”，而是“能组合”：Nikita 的核心论点是，股票上链后可以抵押借贷、质押生息、加杠杆，像对待 BTC 一样对待股票；还能用来对冲锁仓份额（vested allocation）和 PIPE 投资。这是全场最有分量的一个论点。
  3. 激励计划只能拉新，留不住人：Drew、Daria、Zach、Matt 四方一致——奖励能换来短期流量，但留存靠产品、执行速度、流动性、客服和信任。积分/平台币模式被多方点名批评，Zach 明确主张“不要发币，100% 价值以 USDT/USDC 直接返还给用户”。
  4. 蓝筹股代币化对 Web3 吸引力有限，小盘股和 Pre-IPO 才是主战场：Nikita 和 Roman 都认为蓝筹日波动只有几个百分点，不符合加密用户要波动的胃口；小盘股和未上市股权（如 SpaceX、Cursor 案例）才是代币化的增量机会，但监管与标准缺失是最大短板。
  5. 2026 年是“赢家靠分发和信任”的一年：快问快答环节，Mark 说赢在分发（能否接入 Revolut、Interactive Brokers 这类 TradFi 入口），Daria 和 Nikita 说赢在信任与 track record，Zach 说不会只有一个赢家，赢在听用户反馈、抠细节。

一、开场：Pop DEX 公开 Beta 上线

  • Zach Prater 介绍：Pop DEX 结束约 5 个月封闭 Beta，本周正式开放公开 Beta，并同步推出奖励活动 Megapop（交易赚抽奖券的彩票系统）。
  • Pop DEX 定位：永续合约 + 代币化股票 + 大宗商品，全链上订单簿、高速度执行，目前约 66 个交易市场；与一家代币化平台合作（转写为 "Reality"，原文如此），目标 2027 年做到 500–1,000 个甚至更多市场，并自称要进入 Perp 平台前五。
  • 奖励机制：奖励以 USDT/USDC 发放，不发积分、不发平台币；Megapop 奖池分 5 档，现场称已有约 2 万张票在池中，一等奖 77,000（币种未说明，疑为美元）、二等奖 14,000；未来 6 周早期交易者奖励翻倍。

二、为什么 Perp DEX 这么火？用户到底要什么

Daria（机构视角）：Hyperliquid 定了行业标准；现在用户要的是更多品种敞口 + 更开放，以及跨过准入门槛去碰黄金、白银之外的“更 exotic、更有意思”的资产。

Zach（Pop DEX）：用户要三样东西——① 交易执行成本合理；② 平台激励；③ 能直接联系到团队。Hyperliquid 太大了，已无法再围绕用户定制产品；Pop DEX 要围绕用户反馈做产品，搞“100% 价值返还”。

"If your product sucks, it doesn't matter about your incentive strategy because you're just burning money." —— Zach Prater

Matt（Katana）：很多产品在争一个相对停滞的用户池，流动性被切碎；用户迁移只发生在产品真正有优势时（更好的机制、UX、资金效率），激励只是强力的冷启动手段。加密客服普遍比 Fintech/传统金融公司差，这是行业共识的短板。

三、激励计划：拉新有效，留存另有答案

Drew（Arch Lending）：激励能让人来试产品，但留住人的是执行、流动性、客服、UI/UX；否则“你只是花钱买了一次性的数字高峰，然后这些人就去刷下一个奖励了”。

Daria：这和以前的 NFT、Layer 2 热潮没有本质区别——奖励把人拉进来，产品得足够好（UI、执行速度、品种、费用）才能留住；DEX 不会是解决这个问题的例外。

Nikita（Holder House）：投机没什么可羞耻的，大多数自称交易员/投资者的人本质上就是在投机（“想用 100 美元博 100 倍”）。激励和营销驱动的量是市场成熟的必经之路——流动性就是这样一点点攒起来的；但 Meme 币式的纯拉盘不可持续。代币化股票的关键区别在于：股票背后有真实业务、营收和财报。

Matt（Katana）：经历过 Hyperliquid 等多轮空投的用户，和没经历过的用户，行为模式差异很大；激励本质上是“为你想要的行为付费”。Katana 的做法是每两周一次交易竞赛，约 10–12.5 万 USDC 按交易量和盈亏返还给交易者；团队要自问：优化的是活动的盈利性，还是增长？

四、代币化股票的真正用途：对冲、借贷与组合

Nikita（本场最系统的论述）：

  • 他做了 5 年二级市场经纪，撮合过数百笔交易。机构用 Perp 的主要原因之一是对冲：买入锁仓份额（vested allocation）时，在 Perp 上对冲。
  • 同理，PIPE 投资（折价拿到的锁仓股）也可以用代币化股票 Perp 对冲。
  • 代币化的真正效用不在交易本身，而在可组合性：股票代币可以抵押借贷、质押生息、加杠杆——“就像你拿 BTC 抵押再借出再买更多 BTC 一样，现在股票也可以这样”。
  • 蓝筹股不适合 Web3：日波动几个百分点，加密用户要的是波动；小盘股代币化更有想象空间。

No Nonsense Forex（17 年交易经验的内容创作者，非业内人士视角）：

  • 现在没多少人真正对代币化股票感兴趣，平台做这些更多是“怕掉队”——别人都有，三年后你没有就输了；先把东西搭好、把早期错误犯完，等用户慢慢进来。
  • 人们随着年龄增长会对 TradFi 越来越感兴趣，历史经验表明几年后需求会来。

Roman（Piper，Pre-IPO 平台）：

  • 目前市面上的代币化股票大多是 SpaceX 这类蓝筹/热门标的，但散户很难赚到钱；Piper 做的是小盘公司和 Pre-IPO 股权，举例称 Cursor 公司自 Pre-IPO 发售以来涨了 15,000 倍（发言人自述，未经核实）。
  • Pre-IPO 市场规模被严重低估，分析、研究与合规基本是空白；监管澄清预计还要几年。这是他眼中 2026 年最被低估的创新方向。

五、什么激励真正管用？来自一线分销者的观察

No Nonsense Forex（为多家经纪商做推广的中大型 affiliate，自述视角）：

  • 不管用的：期权种类、速度（交易者默认所有平台都有）；平台币赠送（大家默认平台币不值钱，换成稳定币还嫌麻烦）。
  • 管用的两样：① 进入生态后的持续专属感——以 Bybit 为例，生态内全天候不断有促销，介绍奖励太好反而让人起疑；他举例称有交易者随手参加抽奖赢了 2 个 BTC（发言人自述）。② 一对一客服：出问题时能立刻找到真人（他合作的 DEX 提供会四种语言的专属客服代表），而不是不存在的客服、听不懂的客服或 AI 客服。

六、透明度、信任与保险：DeFi 正在补 TradFi 的课

Mark（SpoutFi）：SpoutFi 用客户资产卖期权合约产生收益，必须公开卖了什么合约、在哪卖——“不透明的话，我们完全可以在后台跑一个庞氏骗局而你毫无察觉”。人们现在要求透明度；随着收益预期从 1000 倍降到 2 倍、10 倍甚至 8% 质押收益，可靠性变得更重要。

Justin 主持人提问：Nexus Mutual 这类 DeFi 保险本月刚赔了 500 万美元，FTX 之后也赔过（主持人自述）——人们会不会开始要求 DeFi 提供和传统金融一样的保障？

Nikita：TradFi 来调研的人第一个问题永远是托管人是谁、安全性如何、这些份额在清偿顺序（waterfall）里排第几、公司还有哪些债务。Web3 原住民有点妄想，以为 Web3 是独立于现实世界的另一个世界——它只是技术，像互联网、银行系统一样，更快、更透明、可编程。下一个大机会是：把交易、借贷、质押这些经典金融动作都搬到链上，同时做到透明、有保险、足够安全，再叠加蓝筹股给不了的波动性。

Matt（Katana）：行业正在从“代币化表演”（tokenization theater，只是把资产包个壳搬上链，市场没跟过去）转向“链上市场”（on-chain markets）。BlackRock 等机构的信号让大家认真对待这项技术；TradFi 越来越像加密轨道，加密也在补 TradFi 的保障。

七、2026 年的市场与快问快答：谁会赢？

市场定性：多方认为 2026 年既非牛市也非全面熊市——波动大、走势反复、偏慢、偏熊（Dr. Jack 直言 mostly bearish）。Zach 认为是“好市场”：懂交易的人（Delta 中性、做市策略）照样赚钱，买入持有则不太行。

Dr. Jack（ME Wallet）：SpaceX IPO 的整个打法让他觉得“TradFi 在模仿加密的创新”，像一场加密发行；现在的客户比以前精明得多，能分辨真假，炒作只能带来知名度、推不动基本面；他运营一家 CEX（转写为 seed CEX，原文如此）和自托管钱包 ME Wallet（扫码可直接消费）。

快问快答——2026 年 Perp DEX 的赢家公式：

  • Mark（SpoutFi）：分发。Perp 比期权好理解得多，赢家是谁能打进 TradFi 市场——能否与 Revolut、Interactive Brokers 这类公司达成合作。
  • Daria：信任与 track record。动荡年份最重要的是可靠：平台不出 bug、链不宕机、管理密钥不被盗；信任是日复一日做出来的，不是靠一次发布。
  • Nikita：信任 + 托管 + 保险 + 可组合性（见第六节）。
  • Matt（Katana）：从“代币化表演”到“链上市场”的转变本身就是赢家公式。
  • Zach（Pop DEX）：不会只有一个赢家。赢在把钱返还给创造价值的用户、抠客服与 UI/UX 的细节、听用户反馈改产品。他现场透露：听众中的重度用户 Giveno 提的反馈已被纳入路线图；本场讨论中 No Nonsense Forex 提的一对一客服建议，他当场就加进了路线图。

"We're building this space together, we're growing this space together." —— Zach Prater

  • 他做了 5 年二級市場經紀，撮合過數百筆交易。機構用 Perp 的主要原因之一是對沖：買入鎖倉份額（vested allocation）時，在 Perp 上對沖。
  • 同理，PIPE 投資（折價拿到的鎖倉股）也可以用代幣化股票 Perp 對沖。
  • 代幣化的真正效用不在交易本身，而在可組合性：股票代幣可以抵押借貸、質押生息、加槓桿——「就像你拿 BTC 抵押再借出再買更多 BTC 一樣，現在股票也可以這樣」。
  • 藍籌股不適合 Web3：日波動幾個百分點，加密用戶要的是波動；小盤股代幣化更有想像空間。

No Nonsense Forex（17 年交易經驗的內容創作者，非業內人士視角）：

  • 現在沒多少人真正對代幣化股票感興趣，平台做這些更多是「怕掉隊」——別人都有，三年後你沒有就輸了；先把東西搭好、把早期錯誤犯完，等用戶慢慢進來。
  • 人們隨著年齡增長會對 TradFi 越來越感興趣，歷史經驗表明幾年後需求會來。

Roman（Piper，Pre-IPO 平台）：

  • 目前市面上的代幣化股票大多是 SpaceX 這類藍籌/熱門標的，但散戶很難賺到錢；Piper 做的是小盤公司和 Pre-IPO 股權，舉例稱 Cursor 公司自 Pre-IPO 發售以來漲了 15,000 倍（發言人自述，未經核實）。
  • Pre-IPO 市場規模被嚴重低估，分析、研究與合規基本是空白；監管澄清預計還要幾年。這是他眼中 2026 年最被低估的創新方向。

五、什麼激勵真正管用？來自一線分銷者的觀察

No Nonsense Forex（為多家經紀商做推廣的中大型 affiliate，自述視角）：

  • 不管用的：期權種類、速度（交易者預設所有平台都有）；平台幣贈送（大家預設平台幣不值錢，換成穩定幣還嫌麻煩）。
  • 管用的兩樣：① 進入生態後的持續專屬感——以 Bybit 為例，生態內全天候不斷有促銷，介紹獎勵太好反而讓人起疑；他舉例稱有交易者隨手參加抽獎贏了 2 個 BTC（發言人自述）。② 一對一客服：出問題時能立刻找到真人（他合作的 DEX 提供會四種語言的專屬客服代表），而不是不存在的客服、聽不懂的客服或 AI 客服。

六、透明度、信任與保險：DeFi 正在補 TradFi 的課

Mark（SpoutFi）：SpoutFi 用客戶資產賣期權合約產生收益，必須公開賣了什麼合約、在哪賣——「不透明的話，我們完全可以在後台跑一個龐氏騙局而你毫無察覺」。人們現在要求透明度；隨著收益預期從 1000 倍降到 2 倍、10 倍甚至 8% 質押收益，可靠性變得更重要。

Justin 主持人提問：Nexus Mutual 這類 DeFi 保險本月剛賠了 500 萬美元，FTX 之後也賠過（主持人自述）——人們會不會開始要求 DeFi 提供和傳統金融一樣的保障？

Nikita：TradFi 來調研的人第一個問題永遠是託管人是誰、安全性如何、這些份額在清償順序（waterfall）裡排第幾、公司還有哪些債務。Web3 原住民有點妄想，以為 Web3 是獨立於現實世界的另一個世界——它只是技術，像網際網路、銀行系統一樣，更快、更透明、可程式化。下一個大機會是：把交易、借貸、質押這些經典金融動作都搬到鏈上，同時做到透明、有保險、足夠安全，再疊加藍籌股給不了的波動性。

Matt（Katana）：行業正在從「代幣化表演」（tokenization theater，只是把資產包個殼搬上鏈，市場沒跟過去）轉向「鏈上市場」（on-chain markets）。BlackRock 等機構的訊號讓大家認真對待這項技術；TradFi 越來越像加密軌道，加密也在補 TradFi 的保障。

Dr. Jack（ME Wallet）：SpaceX IPO 的整個打法讓他覺得「TradFi 在模仿加密的創新」，像一場加密發行；現在的客戶比以前精明得多，能分辨真假，炒作只能帶來知名度、推不動基本面；他營運一家 CEX（轉寫為 seed CEX，原文如此）和自託管錢包 ME Wallet（掃碼可直接消費）。

Meeting Notes: Tokenized Stocks Go Live — Does Anyone Still Need a CEX?

  • Original title: Tokenized Stocks Go Live: Does Anyone Still Need A CEX?
  • Date: October 22, 2026 (Thursday), duration 1 hour 16 minutes
  • Host: Justin Roberty, Wen Alt Season (@wenaltseason, a regular Thursday show)
  • Participants: 21,087
  • Link: https://x.com/i/spaces/1MJgNbmvngmGL
  • Featured guest: Pop DEX (Zach Prater); Guests: Daria, Matt/Matthew of Katana, Nikita of Holder House, No Nonsense Forex, Drew of Arch Lending, Mark Ryan of SpoutFi, Dr. Jack of ME Wallet, Roman of Piper
  • Note: Everything below is as stated live by the speakers and has not been verified; the English quotations are excerpts from the transcript and may contain transcription errors.

Key Takeaways First

  1. CEXs will not disappear overnight, but the narrative center of gravity has shifted on-chain: No one in the session claimed CEXs would die. The real question under discussion was whether tokenized stocks and perpetual DEXes can take trading volume and users away from CEXs. The consensus was that the user base is still mainly crypto-native, traditional finance users have not really arrived yet, and platforms are positioning now to prepare for a few years from now.
  2. The real selling point of tokenized stocks is not "you can trade them," but "you can compose with them": Nikita's core argument was that once stocks are on-chain, they can be used as collateral for borrowing, staked for yield, and leveraged — treated just like BTC; they can also be used to hedge vested allocations and PIPE investments. This was the most substantial argument of the session.
  3. Incentive programs can acquire users but cannot retain them: Drew, Daria, Zach, and Matt all agreed — rewards bring short-term traffic, but retention depends on product, execution speed, liquidity, customer support, and trust. The points/platform-token model was called out by multiple speakers, and Zach explicitly argued: "Don't issue a token — return 100% of the value directly to users in USDT/USDC."
  4. Tokenizing blue-chip stocks has limited appeal for Web3; small caps and Pre-IPO are the real battleground: Both Nikita and Roman argued that blue chips move only a few percent a day, which does not match crypto users' appetite for volatility; small-cap stocks and pre-IPO equity (such as the SpaceX and Cursor cases) are where tokenization creates incremental opportunity, but the lack of regulation and standards is the biggest gap.
  5. 2026 is a year when "winners win on distribution and trust": In the rapid-fire round, Mark said the win is distribution (whether you can plug into TradFi entry points like Revolut and Interactive Brokers), Daria and Nikita said the win is trust and track record, and Zach said there will not be a single winner — winning means listening to user feedback and obsessing over details.

1. Opening: Pop DEX Public Beta Goes Live

  • Zach Prater introduced: Pop DEX has ended roughly 5 months of closed beta and this week officially opened its public beta, launching the rewards campaign Megapop at the same time (a lottery system where trading earns raffle tickets).
  • Pop DEX positioning: perpetuals + tokenized stocks + commodities, a fully on-chain order book with high-speed execution, currently about 66 trading markets; it is partnering with a tokenization platform (transcribed as "Reality," as in the original), targeting 500–1,000 or even more markets by 2027, and says it aims to enter the top five Perp platforms.
  • Rewards mechanism: rewards are paid in USDT/USDC, with no points and no platform token; the Megapop prize pool is split into 5 tiers, with about 20,000 tickets already in the pool according to the live session, a first prize of 77,000 (currency not specified, presumably USD) and a second prize of 14,000; early traders' rewards will be doubled over the next 6 weeks.

2. Why Are Perp DEXes So Hot? What Do Users Actually Want?

Daria (institutional perspective): Hyperliquid set the industry standard; what users want now is more asset exposure + greater openness, and the ability to cross access thresholds to reach "more exotic, more interesting" assets beyond gold and silver.

Zach (Pop DEX): Users want three things — ① reasonable trading execution costs; ② platform incentives; ③ direct access to the team. Hyperliquid has become too big to tailor products around users anymore; Pop DEX wants to build around user feedback and do "100% value return."

"If your product sucks, it doesn't matter about your incentive strategy because you're just burning money." —— Zach Prater

Matt (Katana): Many products are competing for a relatively stagnant user pool, and liquidity is being fragmented; users only migrate when a product has a genuine edge (better mechanics, UX, capital efficiency), with incentives serving only as a powerful cold-start tool. Crypto customer support is generally worse than that of Fintech/traditional finance companies — a widely acknowledged shortcoming of the industry.

3. Incentive Programs: Effective for Acquisition, Retention Is Another Story

Drew (Arch Lending): Incentives can get people to try a product, but what keeps them is execution, liquidity, customer support, and UI/UX; otherwise "you're just buying a one-off spike in numbers, and then those people move on to farm the next reward."

Daria: This is no different in essence from the earlier NFT and Layer 2 hype cycles — rewards pull people in, and the product has to be good enough (UI, execution speed, asset selection, fees) to keep them; DEXes will not be the exception that solves this problem.

Nikita (Holder House): There is nothing shameful about speculation — most people who call themselves traders/investors are essentially speculating ("trying to turn $100 into 100x"). Incentive- and marketing-driven volume is a necessary stage on the path to market maturity — that is how liquidity is built up bit by bit; but pure Meme-coin-style pumping is unsustainable. The key difference with tokenized stocks is that behind the stock there is a real business, revenue, and financial statements.

Matt (Katana): Users who have lived through multiple airdrop cycles such as Hyperliquid's behave very differently from those who have not; incentives are essentially "paying for the behavior you want." Katana's approach is a trading competition every two weeks, returning about 100,000–125,000 USDC to traders based on trading volume and P&L; the team has to ask itself: are we optimizing for the profitability of the campaign, or for growth?

4. The Real Use of Tokenized Stocks: Hedging, Borrowing, and Composability

Nikita (the most systematic exposition of the session):

  • He worked for 5 years as a secondary-market broker and facilitated hundreds of trades. One of the main reasons institutions use Perps is hedging: when buying a vested allocation, they hedge on a Perp.
  • By the same logic, PIPE investments (locked-up shares acquired at a discount) can also be hedged with tokenized-stock Perps.
  • The real utility of tokenization lies not in trading itself, but in composability: stock tokens can be pledged as collateral for borrowing, staked for yield, and leveraged — "just like you pledge BTC, borrow against it, and buy more BTC, stocks can now do the same."
  • Blue chips are not suited to Web3: they move only a few percent a day, while crypto users want volatility; tokenizing small caps offers far more room for imagination.

No Nonsense Forex (a content creator with 17 years of trading experience, speaking from a non-industry perspective):

  • Not many people are genuinely interested in tokenized stocks right now, and platforms are building them more out of "fear of falling behind" — everyone else has them, and if you don't in three years you lose; build the thing first, make the early mistakes, and wait for users to gradually arrive.
  • As people get older they become more interested in TradFi, and history suggests the demand will come in a few years.

Roman (Piper, a Pre-IPO platform):

  • Most tokenized stocks on the market today are blue-chip/hot names like SpaceX, but it is hard for retail investors to make money on them; Piper focuses on small companies and Pre-IPO equity, citing as an example that Cursor has risen 15,000x since its Pre-IPO offering (as stated by the speaker, not verified).
  • The Pre-IPO market is severely underestimated in size, and analysis, research, and compliance are basically a blank slate; regulatory clarity is still expected to take a few more years. In his view, this is the most underrated area of innovation for 2026.

5. What Incentives Actually Work? Observations from a Frontline Distributor

No Nonsense Forex (a mid-to-large affiliate promoting multiple brokers, speaking from his own stated perspective):

  • What doesn't work: the range of options, speed (traders assume every platform has it); platform-token giveaways (everyone assumes platform tokens are worthless, and converting them to stablecoins is a hassle).
  • Two things that do work: ① a continuous sense of exclusivity after entering the ecosystem — taking Bybit as an example, there are promotions around the clock inside the ecosystem, and referral rewards that are too good actually make people suspicious; he cited an example of a trader casually entering a raffle and winning 2 BTC (as stated by the speaker). ② One-on-one customer support: when something goes wrong you can immediately reach a real person (the DEX he works with provides dedicated support representatives who speak four languages), rather than nonexistent support, support that doesn't understand you, or AI support.

6. Transparency, Trust, and Insurance: DeFi Is Catching Up on TradFi's Lessons

Mark (SpoutFi): SpoutFi uses client assets to sell options contracts to generate yield, and must disclose what contracts were sold and where — "if it weren't transparent, we could be running a Ponzi scheme in the background and you'd have no idea." People now demand transparency; as yield expectations fall from 1000x to 2x, 10x, or even 8% staking yields, reliability matters more.

Question from host Justin: DeFi insurance like Nexus Mutual just paid out $5 million this month, and also paid out after FTX (as stated by the host) — will people start demanding that DeFi provide the same protections as traditional finance?

Nikita: The first question TradFi people doing due diligence always ask is who the custodian is, how secure it is, where these shares rank in the repayment waterfall, and what other debts the company has. Web3 natives are a bit delusional, thinking Web3 is a separate world independent of the real world — it is just technology, like the internet and the banking system, only faster, more transparent, and programmable. The next big opportunity is: moving classic financial actions like trading, borrowing, and staking on-chain, while making them transparent, insured, and secure enough, and layering on the volatility that blue-chip stocks cannot offer.

Matt (Katana): The industry is shifting from "tokenization theater" (just wrapping an asset in a shell and putting it on-chain, without the market following) to "on-chain markets." Signals from institutions like BlackRock have made everyone take this technology seriously; TradFi is looking more and more like crypto rails, and crypto is catching up on TradFi's protections.

7. The 2026 Market and Rapid-Fire: Who Will Win?

Market characterization: Multiple speakers saw 2026 as neither a bull market nor a full bear market — high volatility, choppy moves, slow, and leaning bearish (Dr. Jack said outright: mostly bearish). Zach considered it a "good market": people who know how to trade (delta-neutral, market-making strategies) still make money, while buy-and-hold does not work as well.

Dr. Jack (ME Wallet): The whole playbook around the SpaceX IPO made him feel that "TradFi is imitating crypto's innovation," like a crypto launch; clients today are much savvier than before and can tell real from fake — hype can only bring awareness, it cannot move fundamentals; he operates a CEX (transcribed as seed CEX, as in the original) and the self-custody wallet ME Wallet (scan a code to spend directly).

Rapid-fire — the winning formula for Perp DEXes in 2026:

  • Mark (SpoutFi): Distribution. Perps are much easier to understand than options, and the winner will be whoever can break into the TradFi market — whether they can strike partnerships with companies like Revolut and Interactive Brokers.
  • Daria: Trust and track record. In a turbulent year, reliability matters most: the platform doesn't bug out, the chain doesn't go down, admin keys don't get stolen; trust is built day by day, not by a single launch.
  • Nikita: Trust + custody + insurance + composability (see Section 6).
  • Matt (Katana): The shift itself from "tokenization theater" to "on-chain markets" is the winning formula.
  • Zach (Pop DEX): There will not be just one winner. Winning means returning money to the users who create value, obsessing over the details of customer support and UI/UX, and listening to user feedback to improve the product. He revealed live: feedback from heavy user Giveno in the audience has already been added to the roadmap; and the one-on-one customer support suggestion raised by No Nonsense Forex during this session was added to the roadmap on the spot.
