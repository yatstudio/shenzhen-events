茄哥：25 号是关键点位，BTC 这波还不算「正经反弹」

来源：X Space《重开一下 关于近期的行情走势 美股 BTC》（2026-11-22 20:44 开播，约 24 分钟） 主持：茄哥 | Pod（@qg7777），单人主讲；直播时约 58 人在线 说明：原场因网络波动中断，此为重开场；主持人全程吐槽网络被攻击、自家网线被孩子碰掉。本纪要据直播期间实时录下的回放转写整理（录音嘈杂、转写有讹误），数字与判断均为其自述口径，未另行核实。

一句话

茄哥的判断是：反弹大概率有，但还没发生——下降趋势线没破、机构筹码还没砸完，11 月 25 日是决定「见底还是二探」的关键时间点；真要建仓就分两次小仓位试，别指望接得到飞刀。

宏观：先加息、后宽松，但 2027 年别指望 QE

  • 他读到的市场预期：12 月加息到 4.00%–4.25% 区间的概率高达 88.6%。若是又急又鹰的加息，短期对市场是摧毁、长期反而是利好——利率见顶回落后，市场会迎来降准式的量化宽松一点的流动性，但他认为 2027 年不会有真正印钱的 QE。
  • 1 月（圣诞之后）是另一个关键窗口：俄乌与美伊之间出什么结果，直接决定油价和能源对立的走向；油价真等完全降下来再进场就来不及了。
  • 美股这边：资金在离场、散户在越跌越买补仓——「这就是资本想要的现象」。他还提到台湾这类小市场已经开始下调融资保证金比例稳股市，本质是变相释放流动性，背后是选举临近怕出事。

BTC：什么才算「正经反弹」

  • 他的标准先看量：4 小时级别量要足够。昨天中午走出一根带量的 4 小时极端反弹；昨晚 8 点那根插针，他后来验证是一个被盗（被攻击）的做市基金在补仓。
  • 破位线别乱认：突破 63,000 不等于上涨趋势，突破 65,000 也随时能砸回去；他自己心里的「真正上涨」线在 74,500，但也承认这个线定得有点高。现状是顶部下降趋势线没破、底部走上行通道，明天（23 号）早 8 点看能不能真正走出突破。今晚没什么交易量，黄金开盘较早，美股盘前要等明早北京时间 8、9 点。
  • 小时线不乐观：走了一个 ABC 回调，RSI 在 59.6 一带没到超卖，MACD 量在缩、慢线要勾头向下——这个位置加仓要谨慎。
  • 机构还没砸完：机构手里还有大量筹码没出来，真要联手砸盘是不惜代价的，像贝莱德必须跑、制造恐慌让散户交筹码；现在恐慌盘交出来一部分，但没交彻底。
  • 极端情形：四开头能看见，45,000 也能看着，但会非常短。他自己的做法是把买单继续往下挂着等——有人在极端插针里买到过 25,000 的 BTC（个别平台），以及 1 分钱的 DOGE、30 美元的 SOL、300 美元的 BNB 这类「便宜货」，但他强调那要靠运气，成交量不会大，平台还可能不认这个定价。

流动性：这轮最大的问题

  • 市场最大的问题是流动性暂时还没枯竭、但在变薄：美股一天只跌 0.5%，不是跌不动，是深度不够——一个大户多单爆仓就能把盘砸穿。这类爆仓很多是连环借贷：拿交易所里借出来的 BTC 做保证金再开大单，资不抵债瞬间归零、连环爆。
  • 做市商也在跑：极端下跌里做市商把钱放进去会被埋（66,000 进去、出来 64,000 照样亏），所以他们撤走后深度更不够，瞬间成交价接不上，就直接打到下面很深的价格成交——底下挂单的人因此得利。他预计 2027 年初（1–3 月）会有一段非常清淡的时期。

操作框架：两次探底、分批小仓

  • 下周 23、24、25 三天要盯紧，可能出现二探见低点；越多人看反弹，越容易被砸出新低。
  • 若砸到 5.1 万一线，他有九成把握确认是底；再往下就是长时间盘整后再看。
  • 建仓节奏：第一次探底买 3%–5% 的小仓位，第二次探底再加到 20% 左右。这一轮从 55,000 反弹到 59,000 太快，接飞刀根本接不到带血的筹码——市场不会给长时间在底部盘整思考的机会。
  • 对普通人的话：4 万买还是 5 万买，平均成本没太大区别，不可能追求到最底，只能买在相对底部的区间。
  • A 股创业板他明确不建议碰（自称不太懂，但认为当前位置危险，让等一等）；DOGE 在 0.05 附近他认为接近底部，极端到 0.045 的可能性不大，但他自己也没加仓；群里听众想的「1 分钱买 DOGE、涨到 3 毛」，他说接不到，除非交易所插针。

The bottom line

Qiega's judgment: a rebound is likely, but it hasn't happened yet — the downtrend line is unbroken and institutions haven't finished dumping their holdings. November 25 is the key moment that will decide "bottom or second dip"; if you really want to build a position, try small sizes in two tranches, and don't count on catching the falling knife.

Macro: hikes first, easing later — but don't count on QE in 2027

  • The market expectation he was reading: an 88.6% probability of a December hike into the 4.00%–4.25% range. If the hike is sharp and hawkish, it would be destructive for the market in the short term but actually a positive in the long run — once rates peak and fall back, the market would get a bit of reserve-requirement-cut-style quantitative-easing liquidity, but he believes there will be no real money-printing QE in 2027.
  • January (after Christmas) is another key window: whatever comes of Russia–Ukraine and US–Iran directly determines where oil prices and the energy standoff go; if you wait until oil prices have fully come down before entering, it will be too late.
  • US stocks: money is leaving while retail investors keep buying the dip and adding — "that is exactly what capital wants to see." He also mentioned that small markets like Taiwan have already started lowering margin requirements to stabilize their stock markets, which is effectively releasing liquidity in disguise, driven by fear of trouble with elections approaching.

BTC: what counts as a "real rebound"

  • His standard starts with volume: 4-hour volume has to be sufficient. Yesterday at noon there was an extreme 4-hour bounce on volume; as for last night's 8 p.m. wick, he later verified it was a market-making fund that had been hacked (attacked) covering its position.
  • Don't misread breakout levels: breaking 63,000 does not equal an uptrend, and even a break of 65,000 can be smashed straight back down at any time; his own "real uptrend" line sits at 74,500, though he admits that line is set a bit high. The current picture: the top downtrend line is unbroken, the bottom is moving in an ascending channel, and tomorrow (the 23rd) at 8 a.m. will show whether a genuine breakout can happen. There is not much trading tonight; gold opens relatively early, and US pre-market movement will have to wait until 8–9 a.m. Beijing time tomorrow morning.
  • The hourly chart is not optimistic: it traced an ABC pullback, RSI is around 59.6 without reaching oversold, MACD volume is shrinking and the slow line is about to hook downward — be careful about adding at this level.
  • Institutions are not done dumping: they still hold large amounts of chips that have not come out; if they join forces to smash the market, they will do so at any cost — like BlackRock, which has to run, manufacturing panic so that retail investors hand over their chips; panic holdings have partly been handed over, but not completely.
  • Extreme scenario: a price starting with a 4 is visible, and 45,000 can be seen too, but it would be very brief. His own approach is to keep buy orders hanging below and wait — in extreme wicks, some people have bought BTC at 25,000 (on individual platforms), along with "bargains" like DOGE at 1 cent, SOL at $30 and BNB at $300, but he stresses that takes luck, the filled volume would not be large, and the platform might not even honor that pricing.

Liquidity: the biggest problem this round

  • The market's biggest problem is that liquidity has not dried up for now, but it is thinning: US stocks fall only 0.5% in a day not because they can't fall, but because depth is insufficient — one large long liquidation can smash straight through the book. Many such liquidations are chain borrowing: using BTC borrowed on the exchange as margin to open another large position, so that insolvency means an instant zero and cascading liquidations.
  • Market makers are running too: in extreme drops, market makers who put money in get buried (in at 66,000, out at 64,000 is still a loss), so once they withdraw, depth is even thinner, instantaneous execution prices can't connect, and trades print directly at much deeper prices below — benefiting those with orders resting underneath. He expects a very quiet period in early 2027 (January–March).

Operating framework: two dips, small positions in batches

  • Watch the 23rd, 24th and 25th of next week closely; a second dip to a low could appear; the more people are watching for a rebound, the easier it is to get smashed to a new low.
  • If it smashes down to around 51,000, he is 90% confident that confirms the bottom; below that, it would take a long consolidation before reassessing.
  • Position-building rhythm: buy a small 3%–5% position on the first dip, then add to around 20% on the second dip. This round's rebound from 55,000 to 59,000 was too fast — catching the falling knife simply could not pick up any bloodied chips; the market will not give a long bottom consolidation to think it over.
  • For ordinary people: buying at 40,000 or at 50,000 makes little difference to average cost; you cannot chase the exact bottom, only buy within the relative bottom zone.
  • He explicitly advises against touching the A-share ChiNext board (admitting he doesn't know it well, but considering the current position dangerous and saying to wait); he thinks DOGE near 0.05 is close to the bottom, and an extreme drop to 0.045 is unlikely, but he has not added himself; as for listeners in the group dreaming of "buying DOGE at 1 cent and riding it to 30 cents," he says you won't get filled unless the exchange prints a wick.
