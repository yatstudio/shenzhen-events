AI Jam | House of Jammers：14 小时马拉松纪要

一句话： 这是一场 14 个半小时的开放麦马拉松——前半段是 AI 与崩盘市场的实质讨论（Scoble、Boardy、再生农业、黄仁勋传闻、黎曼猜想传闻），后半段逐渐滑向数小时的政治争吵与更衣室闲聊；想听干货，听到第 5 小时左右就可以停。

基本信息

  • 节目：AI Jam | House of Jammers
  • 主持：Dominick Romano（@dromanocpm）
  • 时间：2026 年 11 月 21 日 01:31–16:07（北京时间），总时长 14 小时 35 分 56 秒
  • 累计听众：2,112 人
  • 语言：英语
  • 主要发言者：Robert Scoble（前约 4 小时 20 分在场）、Marco、Peter、Ed（再生农业）、Donnie、Webb、Polly 等轮换发言者
  • 备注：本场主持人未开回放，录音为直播期间逐段抓取；约 35 个分片（约 105 秒）抓取失败、散落在全场各处，个别句子可能缺失

结论先说

这场 Space 的主题是 AI，但真正的主线是"崩盘那一天"：BTC 从 12.6 万美元连跌七周到 5.8 万美元上下，NVDA 一天跌 8%、自 10 月 6 日高点跌去约 45%，VIX 当日一度涨 40%。房间里的人一边看盘一边聊，话题在 AI、市场、政治和闲聊之间来回跳。

有实质内容的段落集中在前 5 小时：Scoble 的知识图谱与 Boardy、Ed 的再生农业、以及黄仁勋去留传闻的现场朗读与争论。第 5 小时之后，黎曼猜想传闻、关税长辩、Polly 的游戏、黑客经济学各有一段可听之处；第 9 小时之后基本是政治争吵、GTA 6 与各类 locker-room 闲聊，信息密度很低，本纪要只作一笔交代。

本纪要所有数字与说法均为场内发言者当时的自述或转述，未经核实；其中黄仁勋、黎曼猜想、Lutnick 被炒、SEC 调查等说法，场内本身就有不同版本甚至互相撤回，纪要里会逐处标明。

一、开场：Scoble 的四小时（约 00:00–04:20）

Robert Scoble 是开场的主角，聊了四个多小时才离场。

他的公司与产品。 Scoble 是 Drain Pipe 的 CEO，称公司在制药业站稳脚跟、明年收入翻倍；下周要发一个新 SaaS：云端知识图谱（他类比为云上的 Obsidian）加上"agent 即应用"——agent 自带前端、看板与报告渲染面板。这是他多年梦想的第一个"可卖的假日季 SaaS"，后续还想做模拟、3D、无人机与机器人。

知识图谱与 Palantir。 他谈到 Palantir 的本体论在阿拉伯语等语言上效果差、Neo4j 2022 年推知识图谱，以及 World AI Con Festival 展位轶事。他的判断是：知识图谱是 agent 时代的基础设施，模型本身会越来越便宜。

伊朗空袭与 Anthropic。 Max Tegmark 批评 Anthropic 卷入伊朗学校空袭，Scoble 为 Anthropic 辩护：卫星图是三年前的旧图、学校早已搬出军事设施围墙；Anthropic 曾抵制该用途、为此损失数十亿美元并被列入供应链风险名单。这是他的说法，未核实。

广告业黑话。 他回顾 DoubleClick 2007、AppNexus→AT&T→微软（Xandr）的历史，称 Google AdX 约 15–30% 是无法解释的"暗库存"——机器人网络、一次展示背后塞 15 条广告；同一广告反复出现，是需求碎片化后的填充。理想的广告是"恰当产品、恰当时机、看不出是广告"。

Scoble 的 Grokbot。 他维护 68 个 X list，覆盖约 7 万个 AI 账号，数据库存了 400–500 万条帖子；Grok 每天读 4 万帖、每 3 小时出一份全球 Top 20 新闻报告。他称 X Pro 的 list 是无算法过滤的实时流，还曾用帖库给 NVIDIA 写过一份 80 页投资策略报告。X 流量方面：他平日约 20 万浏览，马斯克转发可到 1,100 万；老号被封前月曝光 2 亿；今年算法改 AI 驱动后，曝光正在拉平。

特朗普与中国做 AI 联合护栏。 Scoble 提到特朗普宣布与中国做 AI 联合护栏——没人想看到车库造生物武器、AI 无人机互相杀戮；但无人机已在乌克兰自主找炼油厂打，误炸风险真实（呼应伊朗学校事件）。

意识辩论。 Azeem Azhar（Exponential View）写"十亿 agent 比一个人聪明"；虚拟人 Trisha Code（AI 音乐明星）：有人克隆 Scoble 的声音形象做了采访，Scoble 称不告诉他是 AI 就认不出来，"这是最差的一次，以后只会更真"；他预测 5 年内人形机器人会聊天、握手、下棋、洗碗，大多数人会觉得它有意识。BCI 方面：奥斯汀一名四肢瘫患者（冲浪事故）戴手环读脑电信号、凭意念控制机械手开合。

自动驾驶与机器人。 旧金山暴雨中 Waymo 遇 3–4 英尺积水停下，被媒体夸大；FSD 在机场接人推理仍弱（找停车位行、找 7 号门接人不行）；农场采摘机器人陷泥里，靠给农民戴头戴相机采集泥地数据训练；数据多了，推理模型 3–6 个月到一年内会补上。

Boardy。 创始人 Andrew（HF0 背景），产品每天与数千创始人/投资人对话，读邮件、日历与短信、撮合人脉——Scoble 称之为"新型约会 app""未来的老板"，估值 1–5 亿美元（他还说了"值数万亿"级别的野心说法），认为 YC 该买下它保住未来几十年的相关性；3,000 多名投资人在上面；还能当约会服务；竞品 backdoor.ai 比较年轻。

Anthropic 员工约 90% 在 IPO 后成百万富翁（Scoble 的说法）；AI 情景喜剧/剧集在谈；虚拟形象做品牌代言无授权纠纷、可规模化；ElevenLabs 式公司已是十亿级。

算力成本与中国。 Scoble 认为算力成本长期只会降（60 年摩尔定律）；微软有新专利可用闲置个人电脑 GPU；机器人要本地算力（Jetson Thor）、模型蒸馏变小、本机运行是趋势。开源 vs 闭源：中国不放弃开源，芯片禁令逼中国练出"在破电脑上写出好代码"的能力， bleeding-edge 芯片最多落后一两年且在加速追赶；美国若与中国开战打不赢供应链；人形机器人若中国造得便宜，人人会买，劳动与税收随之转移。小米在欧洲/非洲遍地、美国一家店都没有；中国车企因特朗普禁中国车入美砍预算，改邀 Scoble 参加 4 月上海车展（称比 CES 还大）。

养老机器人与预防医疗。 90 岁让机器人换尿布保尊严（胜过等护士）；发言者岳母脑瘤、在养老院住了 7 年，两人一间只有布帘。预防医疗的账：心脏病发救护车 1.1 万美元加四搭桥 15 万美元，提前一天预警只需 1 万美元支架；生物标志物预警是"身体的故障灯"；但诊断权仍归医生，责任问题未解。

二、市场崩盘实况（贯穿全场）

这是全场真正的背景音。按场内发言者的说法：

  • BTC：从 12.6 万美元连跌七周，到 5.8–5.9 万美元；有人称 6 万美元的"底"已被撤回（称 2026 年 8 月 21 日公开撤回）；场内一度喊出"44K 正在路上"；矿工关机价约 4.4–4.5 万美元。
  • NVDA：高点 243 美元→现价 127 美元，当日跌 8%、自 10 月 6 日跌约 45%；场内有人预测下周跌破 100 美元。
  • 大盘：两周蒸发 6 万亿美元（发言者说法）；VIX 当日一度涨 40%（后回落至 32% 附近），尾盘到 35；有人预测"周一市场会反弹"。
  • Anthropic：IPO 后跌回接近发行价，场内称流通盘仅约 7%（980M 股），"不算正经 IPO""满是协调操纵的味道"；股价跌到 86 美元（发言者说法）。
  • 个股：Enphase 240→20 美元；Snap XR 眼镜 2,100 美元（Scoble 称很惊艳但不推荐这个价）；Duolingo 91 美元；苹果自动驾驶烧 40B 美元、Meta 元宇宙烧 58B 美元（发言者说法）。
  • 交易员的抄底模型：约 8 个指标当天全亮、情绪"糟得像新冠时期"、中小盘科技跌 40–70%、信贷市场冻结、靠可转债活着的公司面临债务墙；BTC 是金丝雀但近 3–4 年已脱钩；FinTwit 情绪指标（ZeroHedge 引摩根大通数据：FinTwit 约 50 个有影响力的人）；结构性问题是债务（巴菲特"裸泳者"）；增长选项被拿走。
  • 普通人的反应：有人称自己的妹妹本周第一次打电话来，慌张地问 AI 断电、AI 攻击电网与银行的事；Reddit 上出现"刷爆信用卡再破产"的黑帖；有人把离开美国当作退休后备方案。
  • AI 行业的信息传递批判：本该说"生产力工具、把时间还给你"，却说"一切都完了"，结果是虚无主义、怨恨与对数据中心的反弹；第二/第三受众理论——CEO 对投资人说话，公众听到的是末日；IPO 结构是内部人在锁定期满退出、散户是退出流动性。

以上全部是场内发言者的说法与判断，不是本纪要的核实结论。

五、再生农业：Ed 的长段（约 03:45–04:35）

Ed（fieldwork.ai）是全场最成体系的一段发言，主题是食物即药：

  • 90% 以上的农产品营养密度只有应有水平的 10–20 百分位；手机测营养密度仪是可能的产品方向；AI 读不到付费期刊与播客——前沿科学现在靠播客和 Zoom 传播。
  • 遗传学可专利赚钱，表观遗传学对普通人更有益但难营销；GMO/CRISPR 种子"多半失败"，地方 heirloom 种子本地驯化更成功。
  • Laura Kavanaugh 博士（前 NASA 任务控制，后 Syngenta 做 GMO，现投身再生农业）；根噬循环（rhizophagy cycling，植物是细菌的"牧场主"，约 10 年前的新科学）；他称 AI 搜不到该词是"被压制"。
  • fieldwork.ai 自有 AI（喂自家数据）；有人建议用 Bright Data 抓取、WhisperFlow 转写 20 年播客（John Kemp 已这么做）；愿景是一个贯通健康、可穿戴、土地、金融与生物计算的整体再生农业 AI。

以上是 Ed 个人的主张与说法，科学性未经本纪要核实。

一、開場：Scoble 的四小時（約 00:00–04:20）

Robert Scoble 是開場的主角，聊了四個多小時才離場。

他的公司與產品。 Scoble 是 Drain Pipe 的 CEO，稱公司在製藥業站穩腳跟、明年收入翻倍；下週要發一個新 SaaS：雲端知識圖譜（他類比為雲上的 Obsidian）加上「agent 即應用」——agent 自帶前端、看板與報告渲染面板。這是他多年夢想的第一個「可賣的假日季 SaaS」，後續還想做模擬、3D、無人機與機器人。

知識圖譜與 Palantir。 他談到 Palantir 的本體論在阿拉伯語等語言上效果差、Neo4j 2022 年推知識圖譜，以及 World AI Con Festival 展位軼事。他的判斷是：知識圖譜是 agent 時代的基礎設施，模型本身會越來越便宜。

伊朗空襲與 Anthropic。 Max Tegmark 批評 Anthropic 捲入伊朗學校空襲，Scoble 為 Anthropic 辯護：衛星圖是三年前的舊圖、學校早已搬出軍事設施圍牆；Anthropic 曾抵制該用途、為此損失數十億美元並被列入供應鏈風險名單。這是他的說法，未核實。

廣告業黑話。 他回顧 DoubleClick 2007、AppNexus→AT&T→微軟（Xandr）的歷史，稱 Google AdX 約 15–30% 是無法解釋的「暗庫存」——機器人網路、一次展示背後塞 15 條廣告；同一廣告反覆出現，是需求碎片化後的填充。理想的廣告是「恰當產品、恰當時機、看不出是廣告」。

Scoble 的 Grokbot。 他維護 68 個 X list，覆蓋約 7 萬個 AI 帳號，資料庫存了 400–500 萬條帖子；Grok 每天讀 4 萬帖、每 3 小時出一份全球 Top 20 新聞報告。他稱 X Pro 的 list 是無演算法過濾的即時流，還曾用帖庫給 NVIDIA 寫過一份 80 頁投資策略報告。X 流量方面：他平日約 20 萬瀏覽，馬斯克轉發可到 1,100 萬；老號被封前月曝光 2 億；今年演算法改 AI 驅動後，曝光正在拉平。

川普與中國做 AI 聯合護欄。 Scoble 提到川普宣布與中國做 AI 聯合護欄——沒人想看到車庫造生物武器、AI 無人機互相殺戮；但無人機已在烏克蘭自主找煉油廠打，誤炸風險真實（呼應伊朗學校事件）。

1. Opening: Four hours with Scoble (approx. 00:00–04:20)

Robert Scoble was the star of the opening, talking for more than four hours before leaving.

His company and products. Scoble is CEO of Drain Pipe, which he says has established a foothold in pharmaceuticals and will double revenue next year. Next week he plans to launch a new SaaS product: a cloud knowledge graph (he compares it to Obsidian in the cloud) plus "agent as application" — agents that come with their own front end, dashboard, and report-rendering panels. It is the first "sellable holiday-season SaaS" of a dream he has carried for years; later he wants to add simulation, 3D, drones, and robotics.

Knowledge graphs and Palantir. He discussed how Palantir's ontology performs poorly in languages such as Arabic, Neo4j's 2022 push into knowledge graphs, and an anecdote from a World AI Con Festival booth. His view: knowledge graphs are the infrastructure of the agent era, and models themselves will only get cheaper.

The Iran airstrike and Anthropic. Max Tegmark criticized Anthropic's involvement in the strike on a school in Iran; Scoble defended Anthropic, saying the satellite imagery was three years old and the school had long since moved outside the military compound's walls, and that Anthropic had resisted that use case at a cost of billions of dollars and a place on a supply-chain risk list. That is his account, unverified.

Ad industry shop talk. He retraced the history of DoubleClick in 2007 and AppNexus→AT&T→Microsoft (Xandr), and said roughly 15–30% of Google AdX is "dark inventory" that cannot be accounted for — bot networks, fifteen ads stuffed behind a single impression. The same ad showing up again and again, he said, is what happens when fragmented demand gets backfilled. The ideal ad is "the right product, at the right moment, and you can't tell it's an ad."

Scoble's Grokbot. He maintains 68 X lists covering about 70,000 AI accounts, with a database of four to five million stored posts. Grok reads 40,000 posts a day and produces a global Top 20 news report every three hours. He described X Pro lists as a real-time stream with no algorithmic filtering, and said he once used the post archive to write an 80-page investment strategy report for NVIDIA. On X reach: he gets around 200,000 views on an ordinary day, while a reshare from Musk can reach 11 million; before his old account was banned it had 200 million monthly impressions. Since the algorithm switched to AI-driven ranking this year, he said, reach is flattening out.

Trump and China on joint AI guardrails. Scoble mentioned Trump's announcement of joint AI guardrails with China — nobody wants garage-built bioweapons or AI drones killing each other — but noted that drones in Ukraine are already autonomously hunting refineries, so the risk of a mistaken strike is real (echoing the Iran school incident).

The consciousness debate. Azeem Azhar (Exponential View) wrote that "a billion agents are smarter than one person." On Trisha Code, a virtual AI music star: someone cloned Scoble's voice and likeness for an interview, and Scoble said that if he hadn't been told it was AI, he wouldn't have known — "this is the worst it will ever be; it only gets more real from here." He predicted that within five years humanoid robots will chat, shake hands, play chess, and wash dishes, and most people will feel they are conscious. On brain-computer interfaces: a quadriplegic in Austin (a surfing accident) wears a wristband that reads brain signals and lets him open and close a robotic hand by thought.

Self-driving and robots. Waymo stopping in three to four feet of floodwater during a San Francisco downpour was exaggerated by the press, he said. FSD is still weak at airport pickups — it can find a parking spot but not gate 7 to pick someone up. Farm-picking robots get stuck in mud, and the fix is strapping head cameras on farmers to collect mud-terrain data for training; with more data, reasoning models should close the gap within three to six months to a year.

Boardy. Founded by Andrew (HF0 background), the product talks to thousands of founders and investors every day, reads email, calendars, and texts, and makes introductions — Scoble called it "a new kind of dating app" and "the boss of the future," putting its valuation at $100–500 million (he also floated a "worth trillions" level of ambition) and arguing YC should buy it to stay relevant for decades. More than 3,000 investors are on it; it can also work as a dating service. The competitor backdoor.ai is younger.

About 90% of Anthropic's employees became millionaires after the IPO (Scoble's claim). AI sitcoms and series are in discussion; virtual personas can endorse brands with no licensing disputes and at scale; ElevenLabs-style companies are already at the billion-dollar level.

Compute costs and China. Scoble believes compute costs will only fall over the long run (sixty years of Moore's law). Microsoft has a new patent for using idle personal-computer GPUs; robots need local compute (Jetson Thor), distilled models are getting smaller, and running on-device is the trend. On open source vs. closed: China will not give up open source, and the chip bans forced China to develop the ability to "write good code on a broken computer" — bleeding-edge chips are at most one or two years behind and catching up fast. If the US went to war with China, it could not win the supply chain; if China builds cheap humanoid robots, everyone will buy them, and labor and tax revenue will follow. Xiaomi is everywhere in Europe and Africa and has not a single store in the US; Chinese automakers cut budgets after Trump banned Chinese cars from the US and instead invited Scoble to the April Shanghai auto show, which they say is bigger than CES.

Elder-care robots and preventive medicine. At 90, having a robot change your diaper preserves your dignity (better than waiting for a nurse); one speaker's mother-in-law has a brain tumor and has spent seven years in a nursing home, two to a room with only a curtain between them. The math of preventive medicine: a heart attack costs $11,000 for the ambulance plus $150,000 for a quadruple bypass, while a day's warning would mean only a $10,000 stent. Biomarker alerts are "the check-engine light for the body" — but the authority to diagnose still rests with doctors, and the liability question is unresolved.
