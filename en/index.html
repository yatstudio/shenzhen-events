Why verified matters

  • One by one, from the source. Each listing is checked against the organizer's official page — date, venue, fee, registration link. Aggregator reposts are traced back to the original, never trusted directly.
  • Fresh or gone. Events carry their deadlines; expired listings roll off, and changes (new venue, new date, cancellation) are re-verified the same day.
  • Built for agents, readable by humans. Stable IDs, ISO timestamps with timezone, English fields on every record — and a normal web page for every list if you'd rather browse yourself.
