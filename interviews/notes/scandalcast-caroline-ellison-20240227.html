Caroline Ellison：SBF 的前女友与 Alameda 的末任 CEO

片源：纪录片《Caroline Ellison: Sam Bankman-Fried's Ex-Girlfriend》（Scandalcast 频道，FTX Documentary 系列）· 时长：约 23 分钟 · 2024-02-27 发布 说明：本片为旁白纪录片，大量材料取自 Michael Lewis 的《Going Infinite》；本纪要据英文字幕整理、转述片中叙述，人物心理与动机多为片方与书方的呈现口径，并非定论。

一句话

这部片子讲的是 FTX 崩塌里最特殊的一对关系：Alameda 的 CEO Caroline Ellison 是创始人 SBF 的前女友——客户资金被挪用的决策链、两人分手的时间点、以及事后 SBF 把崩塌归咎于她的叙事纠缠在一起；而检方手里最关键的一段录音显示，用 FTX 客户资金填 Alameda 窟窿的决定，出自 SBF 本人。

数学神童与有效利他主义

Ellison 1994 年生于波士顿，父亲是 MIT 经济系主任、母亲是同系讲师，她是三个女儿中的老大。自幼参加全国数学竞赛，斯坦福数学系毕业，安静、爱《哈利·波特》、自认是拉文克劳，大学时在 Tumblr 写博客《World Optimization》，既写简·奥斯汀和 Taylor Swift，也写过「性革命是个错误、女性更适合做家庭主妇」这类与日后女 CEO 形象反差很大的观点——她的斯坦福教授说，很难把认识的那个「很数学」的她与一桩数十亿美元的欺诈联系起来。

毕业后她进了量化交易公司 Jane Street，自称是误打误撞进了华尔街，年薪约 20 万美元，却总觉得自己工作平庸。在 Jane Street 她认识了 SBF——MIT 出身、父母都是斯坦福法学教授——两人因有效利他主义结缘：先赚大钱、再把钱捐出去改变世界。2017 年 SBF 离职创立做套利的 Alameda；一次咖啡馆见面时她正打扮成林地仙子准备去参加角色扮演派对，SBF 向她发出了邀请。她先去问当时的男友是否爱她，得到否定答复后，辞掉 Jane Street 的工作加入了加密世界。她在博客里把自己比作科幻小说女主角：为一家让受压制政权下的人能自由转移资金的数字货币公司工作。

四页纸告白与联席 CEO

核心圈还有 Gary Wang（联合创始人兼 CTO）、Nishad Singh（工程负责人）和 Sam Trabucco。入职第一年，23 岁的 Ellison 给老板 SBF 写了一封四页纸、公文式的备忘录，坦白自己对他有感情、这些感情占据了她大量脑力并影响工作。两人此后多年分分合合。SBF 创立 FTX 并全职去管交易所后，任命 Ellison 与 Trabucco 为 Alameda 联席 CEO——原设想是 Trabucco 管交易风险、Ellison 管人。2022 年两人同登 Forbes 30 Under 30（她 27 岁）。Trabucco 后来辞职离开，Ellison 独自接下人和风险两摊，成为末任 CEO。

FTX 此时如日中天：红杉等顶级机构入股，交易所旱涝保收的模式被投资人比作「押注赌场本身」；2021 年收入超 10 亿美元，SBF 花 2000 多万美元买超级碗广告、请库里和布雷迪代言，29 岁身家约 220 亿美元（峰值约 265 亿），是 30 岁以下全球首富。核心十人住在巴哈马一栋 3000 万美元的顶层公寓里，Ellison 始终待在幕后。

分手、寒冬与 140 亿美元的窟窿

SBF 长期坚持对恋情保密，理由是怕公关与职场偏袒的观感；Ellison 在备忘录里写，她感觉他以她为耻。2022 年 4 月，两人最终分手，她把他赶出了共住的房间——分手正撞上加密寒冬：4 到 6 月比特币跌去一半以上，Alameda 持仓大幅缩水，而它此前从借贷机构借了数十亿美元做交易和非流动性投资（包括回购 FTX 股份、投创业公司），抽贷潮中根本还不上。两人当时几乎不说话。SBF 后来把 Alameda 的亏损归咎于她：分手后沟通变少、且没听他的建议对冲头寸；Ellison 后来承认对冲本会有帮助，但指出 SBF 做了太多无法快速变现的投资也是问题的一部分。

真正的罪在于补窟窿的方式：SBF 决定用 FTX 客户资金替 Alameda 还债。而实际上从公司成立之初，FTX 客户存款就被持续导入 Alameda 账户，用于交易、投资、奢华生活和政治捐款，SBF 还批准了给高管、给父母、甚至给自己 10 亿美元的贷款。Wang 与 Singh 写了软件，让 Alameda 在 FTX 的账户可以保持负余额，等于一条无限信用额度——最初设想上限是不超过 FTX 的收入，很快就开始直接侵蚀客户资金。Alameda 累计从客户资金中借走约 140 亿美元，后来还回一部分、降到约 90 亿，他们盘算着等市场平稳后融资把钱补回去。与此同时，SBF 对外保证 Alameda 在 FTX 平台上没有任何特权、客户资金绝不会被拿去交易。

2022 年 9 月，SBF 写了一份 Google 文档论证应当关掉 Alameda，只分享给 Wang 和 Singh，把 Ellison 排除在外。文档里他评价 Ellison「不是天生的领导者，大概永远也不会是」，主张转而扶持另一家成立不到一年的交易公司 Modulo Capital——其创办人是他的另一位前女友、同为 Jane Street 出身的 Lily Zhang。2022 年第三、四季度，SBF 向 Modulo 投入 4 亿美元，打算用它取代 Alameda。Ellison 在自己的文字里留下了被边缘化的嫉妒与怨气。

九天崩塌

2022 年 11 月 2 日，CoinDesk 曝光 Alameda 的资产负债表：其持仓大头是 FTX 自己发行的 FTT——相当于把自家股票当资产计入账面，对 FTT 价格毫无抵抗力。11 月 6 日，币安 CEO 赵长鹏宣布出售约 5 亿美元 FTT，抛售随即失控，FTT 跌去 75%，挤兑发生；90 亿美元的窟窿让 FTX 无力兑付。11 月 11 日，距曝光仅 9 天，FTX 与 Alameda 双双申请破产，Ellison、SBF、Wang、Singh 均被控欺诈。

认罪、录音与日记之战

FBI 一度找不到 Ellison，外界传言她要逃往与美国无引渡条约的迪拜；一个月后她在纽约出现，2022 年 12 月对共谋电汇欺诈、商品欺诈、证券欺诈和洗钱等指控认罪，同意配合检方指证 SBF 以换取轻判，Wang 与 Singh 随后也认罪。认罪时她对法官说，她真心为欺骗 FTX 客户感到抱歉，她知道那是错的。她面临的指控合计最高可达 110 年监禁，合作预计将大幅减刑。

SBF 则坚称自己不参与 Alameda 的日常运营，并持续把崩塌往 Ellison 身上推：他给一位加密 YouTuber 发去长篇材料，细数她未按建议对冲、回避他的风险询问；他还把 Ellison 私人日记的页面泄露给《纽约时报》记者——法官认定此举构成干预证人，撤销了他的保释，他从软禁转入看守所候审。泄露的日记（存在 Google 文档里）呈现了另一个 Ellison：她自觉不胜任 CEO、热情早已消退，在 SBF 面前有一种「本能地缩小自己、变得安静、顺从他人」的倾向；她也承认分手后是故意减少与他的联系，因为「不给你想要的联系，是我夺回一点掌控感的唯一方式」。

但检方握有决定性的一段录音：在一次内部会议上，Ellison 亲口告诉 Alameda 员工，用 FTX 客户资金去填那个窟窿，是 SBF 的决定。片尾时点（2024 年 2 月），SBF 31 岁，面临数十年监禁；核心圈中唯一未被起诉的是 Trabucco——他在 2022 年 4 月辞去联席 CEO、8 月彻底离开，赶在一切崩坏之前。

后记（片外进展）

影片发布后：SBF 已于 2023 年 11 月被判全部罪名成立，2024 年 3 月被判处 25 年监禁；Ellison 于 2024 年 9 月被判处 2 年监禁。

Caroline Ellison：SBF 的前女友與 Alameda 的末任 CEO

片源：紀錄片《Caroline Ellison: Sam Bankman-Fried's Ex-Girlfriend》（Scandalcast 頻道，FTX Documentary 系列）· 時長：約 23 分鐘 · 2024-02-27 發布 說明：本片為旁白紀錄片，大量材料取自 Michael Lewis 的《Going Infinite》；本紀要據英文字幕整理、轉述片中敘述，人物心理與動機多為片方與書方的呈現口徑，並非定論。

一句話

這部片子講的是 FTX 崩塌裡最特殊的一對關係：Alameda 的 CEO Caroline Ellison 是創辦人 SBF 的前女友——客戶資金被挪用的決策鏈、兩人分手的時間點、以及事後 SBF 把崩塌歸咎於她的敘事糾纏在一起；而檢方手裡最關鍵的一段錄音顯示，用 FTX 客戶資金填 Alameda 窟窿的決定，出自 SBF 本人。

數學神童與有效利他主義

Ellison 1994 年生於波士頓，父親是 MIT 經濟系主任、母親是同系講師，她是三個女兒中的老大。自幼參加全國數學競賽，史丹佛數學系畢業，安靜、愛《哈利·波特》、自認是雷文克勞，大學時在 Tumblr 寫部落格《World Optimization》，既寫珍·奧斯汀和 Taylor Swift，也寫過「性革命是個錯誤、女性更適合做家庭主婦」這類與日後女 CEO 形象反差很大的觀點——她的史丹佛教授說，很難把認識的那個「很數學」的她與一樁數十億美元的詐欺聯繫起來。

畢業後她進了量化交易公司 Jane Street，自稱是誤打誤撞進了華爾街，年薪約 20 萬美元，卻總覺得自己工作平庸。在 Jane Street 她認識了 SBF——MIT 出身、父母都是史丹佛法學教授——兩人因有效利他主義結緣：先賺大錢、再把錢捐出去改變世界。2017 年 SBF 離職創立做套利的 Alameda；一次咖啡館見面時她正打扮成林地仙子準備去參加角色扮演派對，SBF 向她發出了邀請。她先去問當時的男友是否愛她，得到否定答覆後，辭掉 Jane Street 的工作加入了加密世界。她在部落格裡把自己比作科幻小說女主角：為一家讓受壓制政權下的人能自由轉移資金的數位貨幣公司工作。

四頁紙告白與聯席 CEO

核心圈還有 Gary Wang（聯合創辦人兼 CTO）、Nishad Singh（工程負責人）和 Sam Trabucco。入職第一年，23 歲的 Ellison 給老闆 SBF 寫了一封四頁紙、公文式的備忘錄，坦白自己對他有感情、這些感情佔據了她大量腦力並影響工作。兩人此後多年分分合合。SBF 創立 FTX 並全職去管交易所後，任命 Ellison 與 Trabucco 為 Alameda 聯席 CEO——原設想是 Trabucco 管交易風險、Ellison 管人。2022 年兩人同登 Forbes 30 Under 30（她 27 歲）。Trabucco 後來辭職離開，Ellison 獨自接下人和風險兩攤，成為末任 CEO。

FTX 此時如日中天：紅杉等頂級機構入股，交易所旱澇保收的模式被投資人比作「押注賭場本身」；2021 年收入超 10 億美元，SBF 花 2000 多萬美元買超級盃廣告、請庫里和布雷迪代言，29 歲身家約 220 億美元（峰值約 265 億），是 30 歲以下全球首富。核心十人住在巴哈馬一棟 3000 萬美元的頂層公寓裡，Ellison 始終待在幕後。

分手、寒冬與 140 億美元的窟窿

SBF 長期堅持對戀情保密，理由是怕公關與職場偏袒的觀感；Ellison 在備忘錄裡寫，她感覺他以她為恥。2022 年 4 月，兩人最終分手，她把他趕出了共住的房間——分手正撞上加密寒冬：4 到 6 月比特幣跌去一半以上，Alameda 持倉大幅縮水，而它此前從借貸機構借了數十億美元做交易和非流動性投資（包括回購 FTX 股份、投新創公司），抽貸潮中根本還不上。兩人當時幾乎不說話。SBF 後來把 Alameda 的虧損歸咎於她：分手後溝通變少、且沒聽他的建議對沖頭寸；Ellison 後來承認對沖本會有幫助，但指出 SBF 做了太多無法快速變現的投資也是問題的一部分。

真正的罪在於補窟窿的方式：SBF 決定用 FTX 客戶資金替 Alameda 還債。而實際上從公司成立之初，FTX 客戶存款就被持續導入 Alameda 帳戶，用於交易、投資、奢華生活和政治捐款，SBF 還批准了給高管、給父母、甚至給自己 10 億美元的貸款。Wang 與 Singh 寫了軟體，讓 Alameda 在 FTX 的帳戶可以保持負餘額，等於一條無限信用額度——最初設想上限是不超過 FTX 的收入，很快就開始直接侵蝕客戶資金。Alameda 累計從客戶資金中借走約 140 億美元，後來還回一部分、降到約 90 億，他們盤算著等市場平穩後融資把錢補回去。與此同時，SBF 對外保證 Alameda 在 FTX 平台上沒有任何特權、客戶資金絕不會被拿去交易。

2022 年 9 月，SBF 寫了一份 Google 文件論證應當關掉 Alameda，只分享給 Wang 和 Singh，把 Ellison 排除在外。文件裡他評價 Ellison「不是天生的領導者，大概永遠也不會是」，主張轉而扶持另一家成立不到一年的交易公司 Modulo Capital——其創辦人是他的另一位前女友、同為 Jane Street 出身的 Lily Zhang。2022 年第三、四季度，SBF 向 Modulo 投入 4 億美元，打算用它取代 Alameda。Ellison 在自己的文字裡留下了被邊緣化的嫉妒與怨氣。

九天崩塌

2022 年 11 月 2 日，CoinDesk 曝光 Alameda 的資產負債表：其持倉大頭是 FTX 自己發行的 FTT——相當於把自家股票當資產計入帳面，對 FTT 價格毫無抵抗力。11 月 6 日，幣安 CEO 趙長鵬宣布出售約 5 億美元 FTT，拋售隨即失控，FTT 跌去 75%，擠兌發生；90 億美元的窟窿讓 FTX 無力兌付。11 月 11 日，距曝光僅 9 天，FTX 與 Alameda 雙雙申請破產，Ellison、SBF、Wang、Singh 均被控詐欺。

認罪、錄音與日記之戰

FBI 一度找不到 Ellison，外界傳言她要逃往與美國無引渡條約的迪拜；一個月後她在紐約出現，2022 年 12 月對共謀電匯詐欺、商品詐欺、證券詐欺和洗錢等指控認罪，同意配合檢方指證 SBF 以換取輕判，Wang 與 Singh 隨後也認罪。認罪時她對法官說，她真心為欺騙 FTX 客戶感到抱歉，她知道那是錯的。她面臨的指控合計最高可達 110 年監禁，合作預計將大幅減刑。

SBF 則堅稱自己不參與 Alameda 的日常營運，並持續把崩塌往 Ellison 身上推：他給一位加密 YouTuber 發去長篇材料，細數她未按建議對沖、迴避他的風險詢問；他還把 Ellison 私人日記的頁面洩露給《紐約時報》記者——法官認定此舉構成干預證人，撤銷了他的保釋，他從軟禁轉入看守所候審。洩露的日記（存在 Google 文件裡）呈現了另一個 Ellison：她自覺不勝任 CEO、熱情早已消退，在 SBF 面前有一種「本能地縮小自己、變得安靜、順從他人」的傾向；她也承認分手後是故意減少與他的聯繫，因為「不給你想要的聯繫，是我奪回一點掌控感的唯一方式」。

但檢方握有決定性的一段錄音：在一次內部會議上，Ellison 親口告訴 Alameda 員工，用 FTX 客戶資金去填那個窟窿，是 SBF 的決定。片尾時點（2024 年 2 月），SBF 31 歲，面臨數十年監禁；核心圈中唯一未被起訴的是 Trabucco——他在 2022 年 4 月辭去聯席 CEO、8 月徹底離開，趕在一切崩壞之前。

後記（片外進展）

影片發布後：SBF 已於 2023 年 11 月被判全部罪名成立，2024 年 3 月被判處 25 年監禁；Ellison 於 2024 年 9 月被判處 2 年監禁。

Caroline Ellison: SBF's Ex-Girlfriend and Alameda's Last CEO

Source: the documentary Caroline Ellison: Sam Bankman-Fried's Ex-Girlfriend (Scandalcast channel, FTX Documentary series) · Runtime: about 23 minutes · Released 2024-02-27 Note: This is a narrated documentary, and much of its material is drawn from Michael Lewis's Going Infinite. These notes are compiled from the English subtitles and retell the film's narration; the portrayals of the characters' psychology and motives reflect the framing of the film and the book, and are not settled conclusions.

In one sentence

The film is about the most unusual relationship at the heart of the FTX collapse: Alameda CEO Caroline Ellison was the ex-girlfriend of founder SBF — and the chain of decisions that misappropriated customer funds, the timing of their breakup, and SBF's later narrative blaming the collapse on her are all tangled together. Yet the prosecution's most crucial recording shows that the decision to use FTX customer funds to plug Alameda's hole was SBF's own.

A math prodigy and effective altruism

Ellison was born in Boston in 1994. Her father was the chair of MIT's economics department and her mother a lecturer in the same department; she was the eldest of three daughters. She competed in national math competitions from childhood, graduated from Stanford with a degree in mathematics, was quiet, loved Harry Potter, and identified as a Ravenclaw. At university she wrote a Tumblr blog called World Optimization, writing about Jane Austen and Taylor Swift, but also views starkly at odds with her later image as a female CEO, such as "the sexual revolution was a mistake and women are better suited to being housewives." Her Stanford professor said it was hard to connect the "very mathematical" person he knew with a fraud of billions of dollars.

After graduating she joined the quantitative trading firm Jane Street, saying she had stumbled into Wall Street by accident. Her salary was about $200,000 a year, yet she always felt her work was mediocre. At Jane Street she met SBF — MIT-educated, with both parents Stanford law professors — and the two bonded over effective altruism: make a lot of money first, then give it away to change the world. In 2017 SBF left to found Alameda, an arbitrage trading firm. At one coffee-shop meeting, when she was dressed as a woodland fairy on her way to a live-action role-playing party, SBF invited her to join. She first asked her then-boyfriend whether he loved her; when he said no, she quit Jane Street and joined the crypto world. On her blog she compared herself to the heroine of a science-fiction novel: working for a digital-currency company that lets people living under oppressive regimes move their money freely.

A four-page confession and co-CEO

The inner circle also included Gary Wang (co-founder and CTO), Nishad Singh (head of engineering), and Sam Trabucco. In her first year on the job, the 23-year-old Ellison wrote her boss SBF a formal, four-page memo confessing that she had feelings for him, and that those feelings occupied a great deal of her mental bandwidth and affected her work. The two were on and off for years afterward. After SBF founded FTX and went to run the exchange full-time, he appointed Ellison and Trabucco as co-CEOs of Alameda — the original plan being that Trabucco would handle trading risk and Ellison would handle people. In 2022 both made the Forbes 30 Under 30 list (she was 27). Trabucco later resigned, leaving Ellison to take on both people and risk alone, becoming the firm's last CEO.

FTX was then at its zenith: top-tier firms such as Sequoia had invested, and investors likened the exchange's can't-lose business model to "betting on the casino itself." Revenue exceeded $1 billion in 2021. SBF spent more than $20 million on a Super Bowl ad and hired Curry and Brady as endorsers; at 29 his net worth was about $22 billion (peaking at roughly $26.5 billion), making him the richest person in the world under 30. The ten-person inner circle lived in a $30 million penthouse in the Bahamas, with Ellison always staying behind the scenes.

Breakup, crypto winter, and a $14 billion hole

SBF long insisted on keeping the relationship secret, fearing the appearance of PR problems and workplace favoritism; Ellison wrote in her memo that she felt he was ashamed of her. In April 2022 the two finally broke up, and she threw him out of the room they shared — just as the breakup collided with the crypto winter: from April to June, bitcoin lost more than half its value and Alameda's positions shrank sharply, while the firm had borrowed billions of dollars from lenders for trading and illiquid investments (including buying back FTX shares and investing in startups), which it simply could not repay amid the wave of loan recalls. The two were barely speaking at the time. SBF later blamed Alameda's losses on her: after the breakup they communicated less, and she had not followed his advice to hedge the firm's positions. Ellison later admitted that hedging would have helped, but pointed out that SBF's many investments that could not be quickly converted to cash were also part of the problem.

The real crime lay in how the hole was plugged: SBF decided to use FTX customer funds to repay Alameda's debts. In fact, from the very beginning of the company, FTX customer deposits were continuously funneled into Alameda's accounts to fund trading, investments, a lavish lifestyle, and political donations, and SBF also approved loans to executives, to his parents, and even a $1 billion loan to himself. Wang and Singh wrote software that let Alameda's account at FTX run a negative balance — effectively an unlimited line of credit. The original idea was that the cap would not exceed FTX's revenue, but the firm soon began eating directly into customer funds. In total, Alameda borrowed about $14 billion from customer funds; part was later repaid, bringing the figure down to about $9 billion, and they planned to raise money once the market stabilized to make up the difference. All the while, SBF assured the public that Alameda enjoyed no special privileges on the FTX platform and that customer funds would never be used for trading.

In September 2022, SBF wrote a Google Doc arguing that Alameda should be shut down, sharing it only with Wang and Singh and excluding Ellison. In it he assessed Ellison as "not a natural leader, and probably never will be," and instead advocated backing Modulo Capital, a trading firm less than a year old — whose founder was another of his ex-girlfriends, Lily Zhang, also formerly of Jane Street. In the third and fourth quarters of 2022, SBF put $400 million into Modulo, intending to use it to replace Alameda. Ellison's own writings preserve the jealousy and resentment of being sidelined.

Nine days of collapse

On November 2, 2022, CoinDesk exposed Alameda's balance sheet: the bulk of its holdings consisted of FTT, the token issued by FTX itself — in effect counting its own stock as an asset, leaving it defenseless against a fall in FTT's price. On November 6, Binance CEO Changpeng Zhao announced the sale of about $500 million worth of FTT; the sell-off spiraled out of control, FTT lost 75%, and a bank run ensued; the $9 billion hole left FTX unable to pay out. On November 11, just nine days after the exposure, FTX and Alameda both filed for bankruptcy, and Ellison, SBF, Wang, and Singh were all charged with fraud.

Guilty plea, the recording, and the diary war

For a time the FBI could not locate Ellison, and rumors circulated that she would flee to Dubai, which has no extradition treaty with the United States. A month later she surfaced in New York, and in December 2022 she pleaded guilty to charges including conspiracy to commit wire fraud, commodities fraud, securities fraud, and money laundering, agreeing to cooperate with prosecutors against SBF in exchange for a lighter sentence; Wang and Singh pleaded guilty soon after. When entering her plea she told the judge that she was truly sorry for deceiving FTX customers and that she knew it was wrong. The charges she faced carried a combined maximum of up to 110 years in prison, with cooperation expected to reduce the sentence substantially.

SBF, for his part, insisted he was not involved in Alameda's day-to-day operations and kept shifting blame for the collapse onto Ellison: he sent a crypto YouTuber a lengthy dossier detailing how she had failed to hedge as advised and had dodged his questions about risk; he also leaked pages of Ellison's private diary to a New York Times reporter — a move the judge found amounted to witness tampering, revoking his bail and sending him from house arrest to jail to await trial. The leaked diary (kept in a Google Doc) revealed a different Ellison: she felt inadequate as CEO and her enthusiasm had long faded, and around SBF she had a tendency to "instinctively shrink herself, become quiet, and defer to others." She also admitted she had deliberately reduced contact with him after the breakup, because "not giving you the contact you want was the only way I could take back a little bit of control."

But prosecutors held a decisive recording: at an internal meeting, Ellison told Alameda staff in her own words that using FTX customer funds to fill the hole had been SBF's decision. At the film's closing point in time (February 2024), SBF was 31 and facing decades in prison; the only member of the inner circle not charged was Trabucco — he had resigned as co-CEO in April 2022 and left entirely in August, before everything fell apart.

Afterword (developments after the film)

After the film's release: SBF was convicted on all counts in November 2023 and sentenced to 25 years in prison in March 2024; Ellison was sentenced to 2 years in prison in September 2024.
