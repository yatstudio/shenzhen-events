假天才：James Jani 解剖 SBF 与那 300 亿美元的骗局

来源：YouTube 频道 James Jani 纪录片《The Fake Genius: a $30 BILLION Fraud》（2023-09-01 发布，65 分钟） 本纪要据雅加达转写的英文字幕整理，为转述归纳而非原文；片中数字为影片引用媒体与法庭文件口径，未另行核实。影片发布于 SBF 受审之前，结局见文末后记。

一句话

这部片子讲的不只是 FTX 怎么垮的，而是 SBF 这个「人物形象」是怎么被制造出来、又为什么骗过了所有人——红杉一边开 Zoom 一边打游戏的段子、睡豆袋、承诺捐出 99% 财富——最后 James Jani 把镜头转向币安和 CZ：同样的配方还在，FTX 不会是最后一个。

一、神话的原料：2021 年那台「十倍返现的盒子」

  • 片子从时代氛围切入：2020–21 年，加密对普通人就是一个「把钱放进去、十倍拿出来」的盒子；2022 年 2 月超级碗的 Larry David 广告（他对轮子、马桶、投票权全都说「不行」，最后对 FTX 也说「我看不行」——广告反着用这个梗）是狂热的文化顶点。
  • SBF 的个人形象被拆成零件展示：30 岁身家约 260 亿美元，福布斯称除了扎克伯格没人这么年轻赚到这么多；蓬乱头发、T 恤短裤、睡办公室豆袋、开丰田卡罗拉、和 10 个室友合住——影片的判断是，这套「不修边幅」本身就是信任装置，而他最强的资产就是这个「要把财富全捐出去」的有效利他主义者人设（他公开说最后会捐出几乎全部，大约 99%）。
  • 片中唯一的早期逆耳声音：做空者 Marc Cohodes 从 2022 年 5 月起在推特上公开质疑 FTX，还给彭博写邮件请他们调查——当时没人理会。

二、有效利他主义：人设的源头

  • 2013 年，牛津哲学家 William MacAskill 在论坛写下那篇著名的帖子《想拯救世界，别去慈善机构，去华尔街》：与其直接做慈善，不如最大化自己的收入再捐出去——「earning to give」。他提到有个 Jane Street 的学生捐掉一半收入；同年，他在 MIT 找到物理系本科生 SBF 并亲自发展他入伙。
  • SBF 进了 Jane Street（EA 圈子的热门雇主），干了三年半后辞职——Caroline Ellison 当时也在那里，亲眼见他为「可能有别的机会」放弃高薪。辞职后他去 MacAskill 的有效利他主义中心短暂工作，结识了运营负责人 Tara Macaulay。
  • 红杉资本那一幕是影片的名场面：2021 年夏，SBF 在 Zoom 上向投过苹果、谷歌的红杉合伙人讲他的愿景（「我想让 FTX 成为你可以用下一美元做任何事的地方……还能买香蕉」），全程在打《英雄联盟》。红杉领投了近 10 亿美元；一年后、崩盘前两个月，红杉发布 45 页长文《SBF 有救世主情结，也许你也该有》，作者被他说服，认为他可能是世界首位万亿富翁，还特别夸 FTX 的独特卖点是「伦理」。淡马锡、软银、贝莱德、安大略教师养老金都在股东名单里——但没有一家进董事会，影片引投资人的说法：SBF 的条件是少监督，「要投就接受，不投拉倒」。

三、营销轰炸：把脸刷到每个美国人面前

  • 2021 年 3 月，迈阿密热火主场改名 FTX Arena：19 年、1.35 亿美元；电竞战队 TSM 改名 TSM FTX：10 年、2.1 亿美元；Tom Brady 和 Gisele 拿股权当代言人；MLB 裁判制服上绣 FTX 标志；F1、板球、勇士队全覆盖。
  • 最能说明问题的是 Kevin O'Leary：《创智赢家》的鲨鱼，之前公开说加密是「垃圾、无用的货币」，2021 年签了 1500 万美元代言合同后改口说「要说哪里不会让我惹上麻烦，那就是 FTX」。一批以稳健著称的理财网红也接了 FTX 的赞助。影片的评语是：这些人买 SBF 人设的程度，和机构投资者没什么区别。

四、Alameda 与 2018 年 4 月：一切的预演

  • 2017 年底，SBF 与 Tara Macaulay 创办 Alameda Research。名字本身就是障眼法：叫「研究」是为了能开银行账户——真写「加密套利」没人给你开户。
  • 第一桶金是亚洲价差套利：当时比特币在亚洲交易所能贵出约 5000 美元，障碍是资本管制与反洗钱规则；SBF 靠 EA 社群里的一位日本成员绕开部分障碍，又从 Skype 联合创始人 Jaan Tallinn 那里拿到 5000 万美元贷款（EA 富豪圈的钱），美日之间搬砖，赚了 1000 万到 3000 万美元（各方说法不一）。2018 年 1 月窗口关闭。
  • 2018 年 4 月那场内部风波是全片的枢纽：套利结束后 Alameda 开始亏钱，员工把账算在 SBF 的鲁莽交易决策上。EA 论坛上一篇据称出自员工的帖子这样描述他：公然无视法律结构与风控、坚信规则不适用于自己、认为目的证明手段正当。Tara 带着近半数员工当面摊牌——「你必须离开公司」，他拒绝；Tara 和近半员工（很多是 EA 成员）集体辞职。据称他们向 EA 社群高层警告过 SBF 的危险，此事被忽略或没被当真。影片说：这一块拼图预演了后面的一切，只是等人们知道时已经太晚了。
  • 重建的团队全是年轻人：Gary Wang（2008 年数学夏令营认识，技术核心）、Nishad Singh（21 岁，脸书出来五个月）、Caroline Ellison（从 Jane Street 被挖来）、运营官 Andy Croghan（「房间里唯一的成年人」，曾劝 SBF 剪头发，SBF 回答：让人觉得我看起来疯疯的很重要）。这支团队拿去融资的 pitch deck 写着：美国最好的加密交易公司、六个月没有一周亏损、15% 回报「没有下行风险」——与 4 月风波的实情正好相反。

五、FTX 与 FTT：自己印的抵押品

  • 2019 年 5 月 FTX 上线：SBF 控股，Gary Wang 持 10%。影片很早就点破结构性问题：同一个人同时拥有交易所和它最大的交易公司，利益冲突是个「巨大的窟窿」——FTX 凭什么保证不给 Alameda 特权？
  • Alameda 给新交易所提供开局交易量；2019 年 8 月融了 800 万美元；同年 12 月，竞争对手币安也入股 FTX。2021 年 7 月，SBF 用一大笔 FTT 把币安的股份买回——日后 CZ 清仓的 FTT 正是这批。
  • FTT 是 2019 年 7 月 29 日发行的平台币，总量 3.5 亿枚，绝大部分握在 FTX 与 Alameda 手里。影片把它的妙处讲得很透：他们永远卖不掉（抛售即崩盘），但可以拿它当抵押品借钱、在资产负债表上充资产——「魔法互联网货币」，价值全靠投机撑着。2022 年初 FTX 估值已达 320 亿美元。影片还提到一个细节：据称 Croghan 会特意安排来访的投资人「偶遇」睡在豆袋上的 SBF——连邋遢都是设计过的。

六、华盛顿：买下规则的入场券

  • 2021 年 9 月 FTX 拿到巴哈马牌照并把总部迁往拿骚；随后招募前 CFTC 委员 Mark Wetjen——「他是 SBF 通往美国政治与监管之门的钥匙」。入职一个月后 SBF 就坐进国会听证会，成为加密监管的代言面孔，与币安四处被警告的处境形成对照。
  • 政治捐款：SBF 个人两年约 4000 万美元，是美国第六大政治捐款人；到 2022 年底，与他和 FTX 高管相关的捐款超过 7000 万美元。他力推的 DCCPA 法案被业内指控是想让 CFTC 独家监管、给自己造一个法律垄断，Erik Voorhees 在片中片段里直接骂他是「加密界的魔鬼」；影片说当时圈内有一种「不敢反对他」的沉默文化——「目的证明手段」的那一面，又一次露了出来。

七、崩塌的七天

  • 背景先行：2022 年超级碗几个月后，Terra/Luna 五天内从 90 美元崩到 0.72 美元，蒸发约 600 亿美元；三箭资本被法院清盘、Voyager 申请破产保护。SBF 反而到处救火、收购，赢得「加密界的摩根大通」称号——影片提醒：他花的到底是谁的钱，当时没人问。
  • 高管离职潮是先兆：2022 年 8 月 Alameda 联席 CEO Sam Trabucco 辞职，一个月后 FTX 美国总裁 Brett Harrison 辞职，再一个月 OTC 与机构销售负责人 Jonathan Cheeseman 也走了。
  • 引爆点是 CoinDesk 拿到的 Alameda 资产负债表：146 亿美元资产里，大头是自家姐妹公司发的 FTT——所谓两家独立公司，界限原来在这里。CZ 随即发推宣布清仓币安持有的全部 FTT；Ellison 公开喊话愿以 22 美元场外接盘；CZ 拒绝（引其推文大意：离婚了就别假装相爱）。挤兑开始：SBF 发推说「FTX 没事、资产充足」，随后沉默；CZ 宣布签了收购意向书，市场以为得救；仅一天后，彭博与《华尔街日报》披露 SEC 与 CFTC 其实已就客户资金问题调查 FTX 数月，币安以「问题超出我们能处理的范围」退出。破产申请随之而来，SBF 又发了那条逐字拼出「What happened」的怪异推文串。

八、真相：客户的錢去了哪儿

  • 媒体披露 FTX 把客户存款借给 Alameda，规模约 100 亿美元，另有 10 亿到 20 亿美元「消失」。接管破产的 John Ray III（清算过安然的人）在文件里写下那句名言：「在我职业生涯中，从未见过如此彻底的公司治理失败、如此彻底地缺乏可信财务信息。」文件细节：高管用自动删除的软件沟通并鼓励员工效仿；用软件掩盖客户资金的挪用；Alameda 被明确豁免于自动清算机制；FTX 甚至没有会计。另有报道称高管们在巴哈马买了约 3 亿美元房产，核心十人住在同一栋豪华顶层公寓里。
  • SBF 本人选择不沉默：上《纽约时报》DealBook 峰会，称「我从未试图对任何人欺诈」「我没有故意混同资金」；Mark Cuban 的评价是：「我是他的话，会害怕要坐很久的牢。」2022 年 12 月 12 日他在巴哈马被捕；SEC 与 CFTC 的诉状指控他从 FTX 创立之初就把客户资金导给 Alameda，用于投机投资、豪宅和政治捐款，2022 年市场崩盘后又指挥 FTX 追加数十亿美元客户资产去替 Alameda 还债——诉状原话：「FTX 客户资金与 Alameda 自有资金之间没有有意义的区分。」八项刑事指控，最高面临 115 年；Ellison 与 Gary Wang 认罪并转为污点证人；SBF 不认罪，审判定在 2023 年 10 月，他被软禁在父母家中。

九、结尾：把镜头转向币安

  • 影片最后给了普通受害者一段位置（一位客户说：他半生攒下的一切，一夜被扫空），然后落点不在 SBF 身上：James Jani 说，这个故事最糟糕的部分是「我不认为它是最后一个」。
  • 他列出币安身上的同款配方：平台币 BNB 的多数筹码握在币安自己手里；有关联交易公司，其中一家据报从币安美国站收到约 4 亿美元，资金是否来自客户无从确认（币安称报道过时、未作进一步说明）；FTX 之后币安发布的储备证明报告，做审计的公司后来自己把报告撤下，理由是这类报告容易被公众误解、能证明的东西有限；CZ 至今没有明确说过币安总部在哪里；CFTC 已起诉币安与 CZ 故意规避美国法律，司法部也在调查洗钱问题（片中片段提到涉及伊朗的 80 亿美元）。
  • 收尾原话：「你想知道 FTX 故事最糟糕的部分是什么吗？就是我不认为它会是最后一个。」

后记（影片之后的进展）

  • 2023 年 11 月，SBF 被陪审团裁定全部 7 项罪名成立；2024 年 3 月被判 25 年监禁。Caroline Ellison 2024 年 9 月被判 2 年。CZ 于 2023 年 11 月承认违反美国反洗钱法，币安与美国政府达成约 43 亿美元和解，CZ 本人后被判 4 个月。

來源：YouTube 頻道 James Jani 紀錄片《The Fake Genius: a $30 BILLION Fraud》（2023-09-01 發布，65 分鐘） 本紀要據雅加達轉寫的英文字幕整理，為轉述歸納而非原文；片中數字為影片引用媒體與法庭文件口徑，未另行核實。影片發布於 SBF 受審之前，結局見文末後記。

二、有效利他主義：人設的源頭

三、行銷轟炸：把臉刷到每個美國人面前

  • 2021 年 3 月，邁阿密熱火主場改名 FTX Arena：19 年、1.35 億美元；電競戰隊 TSM 改名 TSM FTX：10 年、2.1 億美元；Tom Brady 和 Gisele 拿股權當代言人；MLB 裁判制服上繡 FTX 標誌；F1、板球、勇士隊全覆蓋。

  • 2017 年底，SBF 與 Tara Macaulay 創辦 Alameda Research。名字本身就是障眼法：叫「研究」是為了能開銀行帳戶——真寫「加密套利」沒人給你開戶。
  • 第一桶金是亞洲價差套利：當時比特幣在亞洲交易所能貴出約 5000 美元，障礙是資本管制與反洗錢規則；SBF 靠 EA 社群裡的一位日本成員繞開部分障礙，又從 Skype 共同創辦人 Jaan Tallinn 那裡拿到 5000 萬美元貸款（EA 富豪圈的錢），美日之間搬磚，賺了 1000 萬到 3000 萬美元（各方說法不一）。2018 年 1 月窗口關閉。
  • 2018 年 4 月那場內部風波是全片的樞紐：套利結束後 Alameda 開始虧錢，員工把帳算在 SBF 的魯莽交易決策上。EA 論壇上一篇據稱出自員工的帖子這樣描述他：公然無視法律結構與風控、堅信規則不適用於自己、認為目的證明手段正當。Tara 帶著近半數員工當面攤牌——「你必須離開公司」，他拒絕；Tara 和近半員工（很多是 EA 成員）集體辭職。據稱他們向 EA 社群高層警告過 SBF 的危險，此事被忽略或沒被當真。影片說：這一塊拼圖預演了後面的一切，只是等人們知道時已經太晚了。
  • 重建的團隊全是年輕人：Gary Wang（2008 年數學夏令營認識，技術核心）、Nishad Singh（21 歲，臉書出來五個月）、Caroline Ellison（從 Jane Street 被挖來）、營運長 Andy Croghan（「房間裡唯一的成年人」，曾勸 SBF 剪頭髮，SBF 回答：讓人覺得我看起來瘋瘋的很重要）。這支團隊拿去融資的 pitch deck 寫著：美國最好的加密交易公司、六個月沒有一週虧損、15% 回報「沒有下行風險」——與 4 月風波的實情正好相反。

五、FTX 與 FTT：自己印的抵押品

  • 2019 年 5 月 FTX 上線：SBF 控股，Gary Wang 持 10%。影片很早就點破結構性問題：同一個人同時擁有交易所和它最大的交易公司，利益衝突是個「巨大的窟窿」——FTX 憑什麼保證不給 Alameda 特權？
  • Alameda 給新交易所提供開局交易量；2019 年 8 月融了 800 萬美元；同年 12 月，競爭對手幣安也入股 FTX。2021 年 7 月，SBF 用一大筆 FTT 把幣安的股份買回——日後 CZ 清倉的 FTT 正是這批。
  • FTT 是 2019 年 7 月 29 日發行的平台幣，總量 3.5 億枚，絕大部分握在 FTX 與 Alameda 手裡。影片把它的妙處講得很透：他們永遠賣不掉（拋售即崩盤），但可以拿它當抵押品借錢、在資產負債表上充資產——「魔法網際網路貨幣」，價值全靠投機撐著。2022 年初 FTX 估值已達 320 億美元。影片還提到一個細節：據稱 Croghan 會特意安排來訪的投資人「偶遇」睡在豆袋上的 SBF——連邋遢都是設計過的。

六、華盛頓：買下規則的入場券

  • 2021 年 9 月 FTX 拿到巴哈馬牌照並把總部遷往拿騷；隨後招募前 CFTC 委員 Mark Wetjen——「他是 SBF 通往美國政治與監管之門的鑰匙」。入職一個月後 SBF 就坐進國會聽證會，成為加密監管的代言面孔，與幣安四處被警告的處境形成對照。
  • 政治捐款：SBF 個人兩年約 4000 萬美元，是美國第六大政治捐款人；到 2022 年底，與他和 FTX 高管相關的捐款超過 7000 萬美元。他力推的 DCCPA 法案被業內指控是想讓 CFTC 獨家監管、給自己造一個法律壟斷，Erik Voorhees 在片中片段裡直接罵他是「加密界的魔鬼」；影片說當時圈內有一種「不敢反對他」的沉默文化——「目的證明手段」的那一面，又一次露了出來。

七、崩塌的七天

  • 背景先行：2022 年超級盃幾個月後，Terra/Luna 五天內從 90 美元崩到 0.72 美元，蒸發約 600 億美元；三箭資本被法院清盤、Voyager 申請破產保護。SBF 反而到處救火、收購，贏得「加密界的摩根大通」稱號——影片提醒：他花的到底是誰的錢，當時沒人問。
  • 高管離職潮是先兆：2022 年 8 月 Alameda 聯席 CEO Sam Trabucco 辭職，一個月後 FTX 美國總裁 Brett Harrison 辭職，再一個月 OTC 與機構銷售負責人 Jonathan Cheeseman 也走了。
  • 引爆點是 CoinDesk 拿到的 Alameda 資產負債表：146 億美元資產裡，大頭是自家姐妹公司發的 FTT——所謂兩家獨立公司，界限原來在這裡。CZ 隨即發推宣布清倉幣安持有的全部 FTT；Ellison 公開喊話願以 22 美元場外接盤；CZ 拒絕（引其推文大意：離婚了就別假裝相愛）。擠兌開始：SBF 發推說「FTX 沒事、資產充足」，隨後沉默；CZ 宣布簽了收購意向書，市場以為得救；僅一天後，彭博與《華爾街日報》披露 SEC 與 CFTC 其實已就客戶資金問題調查 FTX 數月，幣安以「問題超出我們能處理的範圍」退出。破產申請隨之而來，SBF 又發了那條逐字拼出「What happened」的怪異推文串。

八、真相：客戶的錢去了哪兒

  • 媒體披露 FTX 把客戶存款借給 Alameda，規模約 100 億美元，另有 10 億到 20 億美元「消失」。接管破產的 John Ray III（清算過安然的人）在文件裡寫下那句名言：「在我職業生涯中，從未見過如此徹底的公司治理失敗、如此徹底地缺乏可信財務資訊。」文件細節：高管用自動刪除的軟體溝通並鼓勵員工效仿；用軟體掩蓋客戶資金的挪用；Alameda 被明確豁免於自動清算機制；FTX 甚至沒有會計。另有報導稱高管們在巴哈馬買了約 3 億美元房產，核心十人住在同一棟豪華頂層公寓裡。
  • SBF 本人選擇不沉默：上《紐約時報》DealBook 峰會，稱「我從未試圖對任何人欺詐」「我沒有故意混同資金」；Mark Cuban 的評價是：「我是他的話，會害怕要坐很久的牢。」2022 年 12 月 12 日他在巴哈馬被捕；SEC 與 CFTC 的訴狀指控他從 FTX 創立之初就把客戶資金導給 Alameda，用於投機投資、豪宅和政治捐款，2022 年市場崩盤後又指揮 FTX 追加數十億美元客戶資產去替 Alameda 還債——訴狀原話：「FTX 客戶資金與 Alameda 自有資金之間沒有有意義的區分。」八項刑事指控，最高面臨 115 年；Ellison 與 Gary Wang 認罪並轉為汙點證人；SBF 不認罪，審判定在 2023 年 10 月，他被軟禁在父母家中。

九、結尾：把鏡頭轉向幣安

  • 影片最後給了普通受害者一段位置（一位客戶說：他半生攢下的一切，一夜被掃空），然後落點不在 SBF 身上：James Jani 說，這個故事最糟糕的部分是「我不認為它是最後一個」。
  • 他列出幣安身上的同款配方：平台幣 BNB 的多數籌碼握在幣安自己手裡；有關聯交易公司，其中一家據報從幣安美國站收到約 4 億美元，資金是否來自客戶無從確認（幣安稱報導過時、未作進一步說明）；FTX 之後幣安發布的儲備證明報告，做審計的公司後來自己把報告撤下，理由是這類報告容易被公眾誤解、能證明的東西有限；CZ 至今沒有明確說過幣安總部在哪裡；CFTC 已起訴幣安與 CZ 故意規避美國法律，司法部也在調查洗錢問題（片中片段提到涉及伊朗的 80 億美元）。
  • 收尾原話：「你想知道 FTX 故事最糟糕的部分是什麼嗎？就是我不認為它會是最後一個。」

後記（影片之後的進展）

  • 2023 年 11 月，SBF 被陪審團裁定全部 7 項罪名成立；2024 年 3 月被判 25 年監禁。Caroline Ellison 2024 年 9 月被判 2 年。CZ 於 2023 年 11 月承認違反美國反洗錢法，幣安與美國政府達成約 43 億美元和解，CZ 本人後被判 4 個月。

The Fake Genius: James Jani Dissects SBF and the $30 Billion Fraud

Source: The James Jani documentary *The Fake Genius: a $30 BILLION Fraud* on YouTube (released 2023-09-01, 65 minutes) These notes are compiled from the English subtitles transcribed in Jakarta; they are a paraphrased summary, not the original text. Figures in the film are as cited from media and court documents and have not been independently verified. The film was released before SBF's trial; for the outcome, see the postscript at the end.

The one-liner

This film is not just about how FTX collapsed, but about how the "character" of SBF was manufactured — and why it fooled everyone: the story of playing a video game during the Zoom call with Sequoia, sleeping on a bean bag, pledging to give away 99% of his wealth. At the end, James Jani turns the camera on Binance and CZ: the same recipe is still out there, and FTX will not be the last.

1. The raw material of the myth: the "10x money box" of 2021

  • The film opens with the mood of the era: in 2020–21, crypto to ordinary people was a box where you "put money in and take ten times as much out"; the Larry David Super Bowl ad of February 2022 (he says "no" to the wheel, the toilet and voting rights, and finally "I don't think so" to FTX as well — the ad plays the gag in reverse) was the cultural peak of the mania.
  • SBF's personal image is taken apart piece by piece: a net worth of about $26 billion at age 30 — Forbes said no one since Zuckerberg had made so much so young; the messy hair, T-shirt and shorts, sleeping on an office bean bag, driving a Toyota Corolla, living with 10 roommates. The film's judgment is that this "unkempt" look was itself a trust device, and his strongest asset was the persona of the effective altruist who would give his fortune away (he publicly said he would ultimately donate almost everything, about 99%).
  • The film's only early discordant voice: short seller Marc Cohodes, who from May 2022 publicly questioned FTX on Twitter and even emailed Bloomberg asking them to investigate — nobody paid attention at the time.

2. Effective altruism: the source of the persona

  • In 2013, Oxford philosopher William MacAskill wrote the famous forum post "To save the world, don't go to a charity — go to Wall Street": rather than doing charity directly, maximize your income and give it away — "earning to give." He mentioned a Jane Street student who donated half his income; the same year, at MIT, he found physics undergraduate SBF and personally recruited him.
  • SBF joined Jane Street (a popular employer in EA circles), and left after three and a half years — Caroline Ellison was there at the time and watched him give up a high salary for "maybe another opportunity." After leaving, he worked briefly at MacAskill's Centre for Effective Altruism, where he met its operations lead, Tara Macaulay.
  • The Sequoia scene is the film's set piece: in the summer of 2021, on a Zoom call with partners at Sequoia — the firm that backed Apple and Google — SBF pitched his vision ("I want FTX to be the place where you can do anything with your next dollar… and buy a banana") while playing *League of Legends* the whole time. Sequoia led an investment of nearly $1 billion; a year later, two months before the collapse, Sequoia published a 45-page profile, "SBF Has a Savior Complex, and Maybe You Should Too," whose author was won over, believed he might become the world's first trillionaire, and singled out FTX's unique selling point as its "ethics." Temasek, SoftBank, BlackRock and the Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan were all on the shareholder list — but none took a board seat; the film quotes an investor: SBF's condition was minimal oversight, "take it or leave it."

3. The marketing blitz: putting his face in front of every American

  • In March 2021, the Miami Heat's arena was renamed FTX Arena: 19 years, $135 million; the esports team TSM became TSM FTX: 10 years, $210 million; Tom Brady and Gisele took equity as endorsers; the FTX logo was stitched onto MLB umpire uniforms; F1, cricket and the Warriors were all covered.
  • The most telling case is Kevin O'Leary: a Shark from *Shark Tank* who had publicly called crypto "garbage, a useless currency," then signed a $15 million endorsement deal in 2021 and reversed himself: "if there's one place you won't get into trouble, it's FTX." A batch of personal-finance influencers known for prudence also took FTX sponsorships. The film's comment: these people bought the SBF persona just as much as the institutional investors did.

4. Alameda and April 2018: the rehearsal for everything

  • At the end of 2017, SBF co-founded Alameda Research with Tara Macaulay. The name itself was a smokescreen: calling it "Research" was how you got a bank account — write "crypto arbitrage" and nobody would open one for you.
  • The first pot of gold was the Asia price-gap arbitrage: at the time, bitcoin traded about $5,000 higher on Asian exchanges, with capital controls and anti-money-laundering rules as the obstacles; SBF used a Japanese member of the EA community to get around some of them and took a $50 million loan from Skype co-founder Jaan Tallinn (money from the wealthy EA circle), shuttling funds between the US and Japan and making $10 million to $30 million (accounts differ). The window closed in January 2018.
  • The internal blowup of April 2018 is the hinge of the whole film: after the arbitrage ended, Alameda started losing money, and staff blamed SBF's reckless trading decisions. A post on the EA forum, said to be from an employee, described him as openly disregarding legal structures and risk controls, convinced the rules didn't apply to him, and believing the ends justify the means. Tara confronted him face to face with nearly half the staff — "you have to leave the company" — and he refused; Tara and nearly half the employees (many of them EA members) quit en masse. They reportedly warned EA community leadership about SBF's danger; it was ignored or not taken seriously. The film says this piece of the puzzle rehearsed everything that followed — it was just too late by the time people knew.
  • The rebuilt team was all young people: Gary Wang (met at a 2008 math camp, the technical core), Nishad Singh (21, five months out of Facebook), Caroline Ellison (poached from Jane Street), and operations chief Andy Croghan ("the only adult in the room," who once advised SBF to get a haircut; SBF replied that it was important for people to think he looked crazy). The pitch deck this team took to raise money read: the best crypto trading firm in America, no losing week in six months, 15% returns with "no downside risk" — the exact opposite of the April blowup's reality.

5. FTX and FTT: self-printed collateral

  • FTX launched in May 2019: SBF controlled it, Gary Wang held 10%. The film flags the structural problem early: the same person owning both the exchange and its largest trading firm makes the conflict of interest a "giant hole" — what guarantee is there that FTX won't give Alameda privileges?
  • Alameda provided the new exchange with its opening trading volume; it raised $8 million in August 2019; that December, rival Binance also bought into FTX. In July 2021, SBF bought Binance's stake back with a large pile of FTT — the very FTT that CZ would later dump.
  • FTT was the platform token issued on July 29, 2019, with a total supply of 350 million, the vast majority held by FTX and Alameda. The film lays out its magic plainly: they could never sell it (selling means collapse), but they could borrow against it and book it as an asset on the balance sheet — "magic internet money," its value held up entirely by speculation. By early 2022, FTX was valued at $32 billion. The film also notes a detail: Croghan reportedly arranged for visiting investors to "run into" SBF asleep on the bean bag — even the slovenliness was designed.

6. Washington: buying a ticket to write the rules

  • In September 2021, FTX obtained a Bahamas license and moved its headquarters to Nassau; it then recruited former CFTC commissioner Mark Wetjen — "he was SBF's key to American politics and the regulators." A month into the job, SBF was sitting in a congressional hearing as the public face of crypto regulation, in stark contrast to Binance being warned everywhere.
  • Political donations: SBF personally gave about $40 million over two years, the sixth-largest political donor in America; by the end of 2022, donations connected to him and FTX executives exceeded $70 million. The DCCPA bill he championed was accused within the industry of trying to give the CFTC exclusive oversight and build himself a legal monopoly; Erik Voorhees, in a clip in the film, calls him "the devil of crypto" to his face. The film says there was a culture of silence in the industry at the time — "don't dare oppose him" — the "ends justify the means" side surfacing once again.

7. The seven days of the collapse

  • First the backdrop: a few months after the 2022 Super Bowl, Terra/Luna collapsed from $90 to $0.72 in five days, wiping out about $60 billion; Three Arrows Capital was liquidated by court order and Voyager filed for bankruptcy protection. SBF, by contrast, was rescuing and acquiring everywhere, earning the title "the J.P. Morgan of crypto" — the film reminds us: whose money he was spending, nobody asked at the time.
  • The wave of executive departures was the omen: Alameda co-CEO Sam Trabucco resigned in August 2022; a month later FTX US president Brett Harrison resigned; a month after that, OTC and institutional sales head Jonathan Cheeseman left too.
  • The trigger was the Alameda balance sheet obtained by CoinDesk: of $14.6 billion in assets, the bulk was FTT issued by its own sister company — so much for the two "independent" companies; this was where the line actually was. CZ immediately tweeted that Binance would liquidate all the FTT it held; Ellison publicly offered to buy it over the counter at $22; CZ refused (the gist of his tweet: don't pretend to be in love after the divorce). The bank run began: SBF tweeted that "FTX is fine, assets are fine," then went silent; CZ announced a signed letter of intent to acquire, and the market thought it was saved; just a day later, Bloomberg and the *Wall Street Journal* revealed that the SEC and CFTC had in fact been investigating FTX over customer funds for months, and Binance walked away, saying the problems were "beyond our ability to help." The bankruptcy filing followed, and SBF posted the bizarre tweet thread spelling out "What happened" letter by letter.

8. The truth: where the customers' money went

  • Media reported that FTX had lent customer deposits to Alameda, on the order of $10 billion, with another $1 billion to $2 billion "missing." John Ray III, who took over the bankruptcy (the man who liquidated Enron), wrote the famous line in a filing: "Never in my career have I seen such a complete failure of corporate controls and such a complete absence of trustworthy financial information." Details from the filings: executives communicated using auto-deleting software and encouraged employees to do the same; software was used to conceal the misuse of customer funds; Alameda was explicitly exempted from the auto-liquidation mechanism; FTX didn't even have accountants. Other reports said the executives bought about $300 million of real estate in the Bahamas, with the core ten living in the same luxury penthouse.
  • SBF himself chose not to stay silent: at the *New York Times* DealBook summit he said "I never tried to commit fraud on anyone" and "I didn't knowingly commingle funds"; Mark Cuban's assessment: "If I were him, I'd be scared of going to jail for a long time." On December 12, 2022, he was arrested in the Bahamas; the SEC and CFTC complaints alleged that from FTX's founding he had funneled customer funds to Alameda for speculative investments, luxury homes and political donations, and that after the 2022 market crash he directed FTX to send billions more in customer assets to repay Alameda's debts — in the complaint's own words: "there was no meaningful distinction between FTX customer funds and Alameda's own funds." Eight criminal charges, facing up to 115 years; Ellison and Gary Wang pleaded guilty and turned state's witnesses; SBF pleaded not guilty, with trial set for October 2023, under house arrest at his parents' home.

9. The ending: turning the camera on Binance

  • The film finally gives ordinary victims a place (one customer says: everything he had saved over half a lifetime was wiped out overnight), and then the point lands not on SBF: James Jani says the worst part of this story is "I don't think it's the last one."
  • He lists the same recipe at Binance: the majority of the platform token BNB is held by Binance itself; there are affiliated trading firms, one of which reportedly received about $400 million from Binance US, with no way to confirm whether the money came from customers (Binance called the reporting outdated and declined to elaborate); the proof-of-reserves report Binance published after FTX — the auditing firm later withdrew its own report, on the grounds that such reports are easily misunderstood by the public and can prove only so much; CZ still has never clearly said where Binance's headquarters is; the CFTC has sued Binance and CZ for deliberately evading US law, and the Department of Justice is also investigating money laundering (a clip in the film mentions $8 billion connected to Iran).
  • The closing line, verbatim: "You want to know what the worst part of the FTX story is? It's that I don't think it will be the last one."

Postscript (what happened after the film)

  • In November 2023, SBF was convicted by a jury on all 7 counts; in March 2024 he was sentenced to 25 years in prison. Caroline Ellison was sentenced to 2 years in September 2024. CZ pleaded guilty in November 2023 to violating US anti-money-laundering law; Binance reached a settlement of about $4.3 billion with the US government, and CZ himself was later sentenced to 4 months.
